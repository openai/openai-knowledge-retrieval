# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)
- Who: For Advisors, Sales, RMs, Underwriters, Support engaging clients seeking investment research, models, or bespoke insights.
- What: Standardized research methods, deliverables, pricing, and compliance controls. Covers equity, fixed income, multi-asset, and ESG research.
- When:
  - Standard research request SLA: 1 business day triage; 3 business days delivery (RCH-STD).
  - Premium/bespoke SLA: scoped within 2 business days; delivery per SOW, typically 5–10 business days.
  - Inquiry response SLA: 4 business hours for tiered clients (RCH-PRO+); 1 business day standard.
- Where: Submit via Atlas CRM > Research Request [OBJ: RESQ], or ServiceNow > Research Intake [CAT: GRI-RCH].
- Do:
  - Use approved talking points and disclosures (see Section 7).
  - Log every research touchpoint in Atlas CRM with ResearchMethodID and DataSource tags.
  - Route bespoke work via Deal Desk workflow and Approval Matrix (Section 6).
- Don’t:
  - Provide investment recommendations without suitability context (NFG-KYC-0110).
  - Share draft research externally or circumvent NRH watermarking.
  - Promise performance or undisclosed access to restricted analysts.
- Systems RTO/RPO:
  - NRH RTO: 4 hours; RPO: 15 minutes. Atlas CRM RTO: 2 hours; RPO: near-real-time (NRT).
- Escalations: Research Triage Lead on-call (Slack #gri-triage, pager 555-0199); Compliance escalation via ComplyTrack ticket CT-RESEARCH-ESC.

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases
- Private Wealth Clients (PWM)
  - Needs: Thematic equity notes, muni bond ladders, ESG screens aligned to values.
  - Fit: RCH-STD, RCH-PRO for model overlays; bespoke values-alignment memo (≤10 hrs).
  - Non-Fit: Quant code delivery, backtest IP transfer.
- Institutional (Asset Owners, Endowments)
  - Needs: Macro outlook, sector deep-dives, factor exposures, policy impact.
  - Fit: RCH-PRO, RCH-BSPK SOWs; analyst workshops.
  - Non-Fit: Exclusive access to material nonpublic information (MNPI) — prohibited.
- Middle-Market Corporates/Treasury
  - Needs: Cash management, short-duration fixed income research, rating outlooks.
  - Fit: RCH-STD notes; RCH-PRO access to credit model summaries.
  - Non-Fit: Ratings advice as if NRSRO; we provide research, not ratings.
- Intermediaries (RIAs/BDs via Platforms)
  - Needs: White-labeled research packets, model commentary, data-feed summaries.
  - Fit: RCH-PRO platform licensing; API pull from NRH.
  - Non-Fit: Unlimited redistribution without license extension.

Eligibility assumptions:
- KYC/KYB completed (NFG-KYC-0110).
- Research entitlements configured in NRH (roles: STD_VIEW, PRO_VIEW, BSPK_CLIENT).
- MiFID II unbundling flagged for UK/EU entities; separate research invoice setup.

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions
BANT/CHAMP-aligned prompts:
- Budget: “Is your research funded via execution commissions or hard-dollar budget? For UK/EU, do you require separate research invoicing under MiFID II?”
- Authority: “Who signs off on research subscriptions and bespoke SOWs? Any procurement thresholds we should know?”
- Need: “Which asset classes and time horizons are critical? Are you after single-name deep dives, macro themes, factor tilts, or ESG controversy monitoring?”
- Timing: “What is your cadence for investment committee meetings? Do you need pre-read packets on a fixed schedule?”
- Metrics: “How will you measure research impact—alpha capture, risk reduction, or governance compliance?”
- Red flags:
  - Requests for MNPI or pre-publication drafts.
  - Pressure to alter ratings for deal considerations (trigger COI protocol).
  - Promise-seeking (“guaranteed outperformance”) or exclusive distribution rights without license.

# 4. Product/Policy Overview
Core research methods and deliverables:
- Equity Research (Fundamental)
  - Methods: DCF, EVA, relative multiples, channel checks using compliant public sources; factor overlays (quality, value, momentum).
  - Deliverables: Initiation notes, earnings previews/reviews, valuation models (PDF only; spreadsheets restricted to summary).
  - Limits: No raw expert network transcripts. No MNPI. Model spreadsheets require RMC approval.
- Fixed Income Research
  - Methods: Spread decomposition, credit migration matrices, scenario analysis (rate shocks ±100/200 bps), liquidity scoring (NFG-LIQ-Score v2.1).
  - Deliverables: Sector outlooks, issuer summaries, ladder construction guidance, stress-test exhibits.
- Multi-Asset & Macro
  - Methods: Regime classification (HML-VOL map), drawdown probability via block bootstrap, policy event trees.
  - Deliverables: Quarterly outlook, tactical tilts memo, risk heatmaps.
- ESG Research
  - Methods: SASB materiality mapping, controversy scoring (NFG-ESG-CV v3.0), UN SDG alignment tags.
  - Deliverables: ESG tear sheets, exclusion lists, stewardship talking points.
- Data Quality and Governance
  - Sources: Bloomberg, FactSet, MSCI ESG, internal DataLake DLK-SF1.
  - Controls: Source tagging in NRH, analyst sign-off, compliance review via ComplyTrack workflow.
- Archival and Retention
  - NRH auto-archives to SEC-17a-4 storage; retention 7 years per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.
- Conflicts Management
  - Wall-crossing protocol NFG-COI-0901; research independence statement appended to all notes.

See also: RES-2025-0003 (Equity Research Ratings & Models), RES-2025-0009 (ESG Scoring Framework).

# 5. Pricing, Fees, and Discounting
Research subscriptions (annual, per legal entity):
- RCH-STD: $18,000 — access to published notes, monthly webinars, email Q&A (best effort).
- RCH-PRO: $65,000 — STD plus priority Q&A (SLA 4 business hours), model summaries, analyst office hours (2x/month).
- RCH-BSPK: SOW-based, starting at $15,000 per engagement; rate card $350/hour analyst, $500/hour senior analyst, $800/hour sector head.

Discounting guardrails:
- Volume tier: 2–4 entities: 10% off; 5+ entities: 15% off (US only). No stacking beyond 20%.
- Strategic logo discount up to 15% with Marketing VP approval.
- MiFID II markets: discounts prohibited that tie to execution; hard-dollar only.

Approval Matrix (Pricing/Contracting):
- Level 0: List price, standard MSA — Advisor/RM may close.
- Level 1: ≤10% discount or ≤$50,000 bespoke SOW — Deal Desk (Marco Diaz) approval within 1 business day.
- Level 2: 11–20% discount or $50,001–$150,000 SOW — Research Finance Controller + Compliance sign-off; SLA 2 business days.
- Level 3: >20% discount or >$150,000 SOW, white-label rights, data feed redistribution — RMC approval; SLA 5 business days.

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow
Step-by-step with SLAs and artifacts:
1) Intake
- Create Atlas CRM Research Opportunity [OPP Type: Research] and link a Research Request [RESQ].
- Required artifacts: Client need summary, persona, regions, MiFID flag, data sources required.
- SLA: Triage within 1 business day by Research Triage Lead.

2) Scoping Call
- Participants: Advisor/RM, Lead Analyst, Deal Desk, Compliance (if EU/UK).
- Output: Scope of Work (SOW) draft with deliverables, IP/use rights, SLAs, pricing.
- SLA: Schedule within 2 business days of intake.

3) Risk & Compliance Review
- ComplyTrack workflow CT-RVW-RE-Methods: review for MNPI risk, redistribution rights, and geographic compliance.
- SLA: 2 business days. Evidence: checklist signed by Compliance reviewer in ComplyTrack.

4) Pricing & Approvals
- Apply Approval Matrix (Section 5). Capture approvals in Atlas CRM [APP records] with approver IDs.
- SLA: per matrix. Evidence: approval email attached or ComplyTrack approval log.

5) Contracting
- Generate Order Form using DocGen template RCH-OF-2025; attach T&Cs RID: RCH-TC-2025.
- For EU/UK: add Research Annex RA-24 (MiFID II hard-dollar terms).
- SLA: Legal turnaround 3 business days standard.

6) Delivery & Onboarding
- Entitle users in NRH to appropriate roles; confirm watermarking is enabled.
- Schedule kickoff and delivery cadence. Record dates in Atlas CRM [OBJ: DELV].
- SLA: Entitlements within 1 business day post-signature.

7) Post-Delivery QA
- QA checklist in NRH: verify source tags, disclosures, and archive ID.
- SLA: 24 hours from publication. Evidence: NRH Publication Log entry.

Escalation path:
- Triage issues: Research Triage Lead (on-call).
- Pricing disputes: Deal Desk Lead.
- Compliance holds: Compliance Advisory Director.
- System outages: NRH Support via SNOW INC queue (RTO 4h).

# 7. Risk and Compliance Disclosures
Required statements in conversations and documents:
- Independence: “NFG Research is produced independently of Investment Banking and Sales & Trading. Opinions may differ from other NFG units.”
- No MNPI: “We do not share unpublished, material information. Research is based on public or appropriately licensed sources.”
- Suitability: “This research is not a personal recommendation and does not consider your specific objectives or constraints. Consult your advisor.”
- MiFID II (EU/UK): “Research is provided on a hard-dollar basis and is not contingent on trade execution.”
- Conflicts: “See NFG Conflict Disclosures at https://nfg.local/disclosures/coi.”
- Reproduction: “Redistribution requires written permission or a redistribution license.”
- Archival: “All published research is archived per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.”

# 10. Procedures
A) Onboarding a New Research Subscriber (RCH-PRO)
1. Verify KYC/KYB completion in Atlas CRM; control: field KYCStatus=Verified; evidence: KYC Doc ID.
2. Confirm MiFIDFlag and BillingMode; control: validation rule fires if misaligned; evidence: CRM validation log.
3. Submit entitlements in NRH Admin; control: role must equal PRO_VIEW; evidence: NRH Entitlement Ticket EN-2025-3312.
4. Send welcome packet (Doc RCH-WP-2025) including disclosures and portal guide; evidence: email logged with MessageID.
5. Schedule analyst office hours; control: calendar invite with RM and client; evidence: CAL-2025-8842.
6. QA check watermarking on sample note; evidence: screenshot stored in Atlas [DOC-IMG-1142].
SLA: Complete within 1 business day of contract signature.

B) Publishing a Research Note
1. Draft in NRH Composer; tag sources; control: required field DataSources non-empty.
2. Internal peer review; control: at least one AnalystPeer reviewer; evidence: NRH Review Log entry.
3. Compliance review via ComplyTrack; control: CT checklist completed; evidence: CT-REV-ID.
4. Assign disclosures; control: standard and issuer-specific; evidence: appended block.
5. Publish; control: ArchiveID generated; evidence: NRH-ARC ID.
6. Distribute to entitled users only; control: NRH entitlement check; evidence: Email campaign ID RCH-BLT-2025-221.
SLA: Draft to publish in 3 business days for standard notes.

C) Handling a Bespoke Request
1. Intake via POST /v1/research/requests or Atlas RESQ; control: fields complete.
2. Scope with client; control: SOW template RCH-SOW-2025 used; evidence: Doc ID.
3. Run COI check; control: ComplyTrack case CT-COI-XXXXX; evidence: approval stamp.
4. Estimate hours and price; control: rate card applied; evidence: pricing worksheet PW-2025-##.
5. Route for approval per matrix; evidence: APP-XXXX records.
6. Execute work; control: method log maintained (see D).
7. Deliverables via NRH with watermark; evidence: ArchiveID.
SLA: Scope within 2 business days; delivery per SOW.

D) Methodology Logging (Audit Trail)
- Maintain a Method Log for bespoke deliverables:
  - MethodLogID, DataSources, ModelsUsed (e.g., DCF-v4.2), Assumptions, AnalystIDs, DateTimeUTC.
- Stored in NRH under the deliverable; immutable post-publication.
- Evidence example:
  {
    "methodLogId": "ML-2025-00412",
    "dataSources": ["BBG","MSCI","DLK-SF1"],
    "modelsUsed": ["DCF-v4.2","ESG-CV v3.0"],
    "assumptions": ["WACC 8.2%","LTG 2.5%","ESG penalty beta -0.03"],
    "analystIds": ["user:skline","user:tmori"],
    "timestamp": "2025-10-07T14:11:22Z"
  }

Control checks:
- No publication without ComplianceReviewID.
- If DataSources includes “Expert Network” then hard block; escalation to Compliance.

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix
- “We get research bundled with execution. Why pay?”
  - Response: “In UK/EU, MiFID II requires hard-dollar separation. In US, our PRO tier adds priority access, analyst hours, and audit-ready documentation of methods and sources.”
- “We need preliminary drafts.”
  - Response: “We cannot share drafts externally. We can schedule a post-publication debrief and customize a follow-up memo compliant with our policies.”
- “Guarantee this model will outperform.”
  - Response: “We don’t guarantee performance. Our methods are transparent, independently reviewed, and stress-tested across regimes.”
- “Give us the raw model code/data.”
  - Response: “We provide summary outputs and methodology. Raw code and proprietary datasets aren’t licensed for transfer, but we can add fields or create tailored exhibits.”
- “We need redistribution rights to our 500 sub-advisors.”
  - Response: “Redistribution is available via a license and requires Level 3 approval. We’ll scope it and ensure proper watermarking and tracking.”

# 14. Regional Variations
- US/CA: Commission-funded research permitted; ensure disclosures clarify separation of banking and research. FINRA 2210 content standards apply.
- UK/EU: MiFID II unbundling; research must be hard-dollar and separately invoiced. Add Research Annex RA-24. No hybrid billing. UK COBS 12 on investment research applies.
- APAC: Country-specific rules vary; treat as US-like unless flagged by Compliance; China/HK distribution through licensed entities only; pre-clear with ComplyTrack country module.

Rollover nuances:
- VAT: UK/EU invoices may require VAT on research services; coordinate with Finance.
- Data residency: EU personal data must not be stored outside EU in free-text notes. Avoid PII in NRH content.
