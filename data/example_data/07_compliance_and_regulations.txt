# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)
- Who: All frontline staff must follow this guide for prospecting, onboarding, cross-sell, renewals, and service changes.
- What: KYC/AML, sanctions/PEP, suitability, privacy/consent, and record-retention controls that must be completed before account activation, product upgrades, or large transactions.
- When:
  - Initial KYC: within 8 business hours of receiving complete documents (SLA).
  - Sanctions/PEP screening: within 2 business hours (SLA) pre-trade or pre-funding.
  - Record archival: within 24 hours of creation to Sentinel WORM (RPO 0, RTO 4h).
  - Periodic review: KYC Tier 1 annually; Tier 2 semi-annually; Tier 3 quarterly.
- Where: Submit via Nexus360 case “COM-ONB” and Quorum DD for exceptions.
- Do:
  - Use approved scripts for disclosures and consent.
  - Capture UBO ≥25% and control prong for entities.
  - Log all client communications to VaultEdge and tag retention class.
- Don’t:
  - Don’t send PII via email; use SecureShare link in Nexus360.
  - Don’t override sanctions hits without Compliance approval (L2+).
  - Don’t promise timelines shorter than SLA without Quorum DD exception.

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases
- Retail HNW Individual (Wealth)
  - Use case: Managed accounts, structured notes.
  - Fit: Government-issued ID, proof of address, passes sanctions/PEP. Suitability evidence for complex products.
  - Non-fit: Refuses source-of-funds or politically exposed without EDD tolerance.
- Corporate (LLC/Inc.)
  - Use case: Treasury, FX hedging, custody.
  - Fit: KYB docs, UBO disclosure, board resolution. Clear ownership chain.
  - Non-fit: Bearer shares, complex offshore chains with no verifiable UBO.
- Financial Institution (FI)
  - Use case: Correspondent services.
  - Fit: Global intermediary certification, AML attestation, Wolfsberg questionnaire.
  - Non-fit: High-risk jurisdictions (OFAC comprehensive sanctions).
- Non-Profit/Charity
  - Use case: Donation management, custody.
  - Fit: Tax-exempt certification, mission alignment.
  - Non-fit: Inconsistent funding sources; negative media on diversion of funds.

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions
- Business intent: “Describe the purpose, expected volumes, and counterparties.” Red flag: vague or cash-intensive without rationale.
- Identity: “Who owns/controls 25%+? Any senior managing officials?” Red flag: reluctance to name UBOs.
- Geography: “Where are you incorporated and operating?” Red flag: operations in sanctioned regions.
- Source of funds/wealth: “What is the origin of the funds?” Red flag: third-party funding with no contract.
- Expected activity: “Monthly deposits, wires, jurisdictions?” Red flag: high-risk corridors with no ties.
- Politically exposed: “Any PEP affiliations?” Red flag: recent appointment or close associate without transparency.
- Data privacy: “Can we capture and store your consent preferences?” Red flag: refusal while requesting digital services.
- Suitability: “Experience with derivatives/leverage?” Red flag: no experience but seeking complex products.

# 4. Product/Policy Overview
- Capabilities:
  - KYC/AML: Automated identity/KYB in Veriscope KYC; EDD available (COMPLIANCE-SVC-02).
  - Sanctions/PEP: AtlasScreen daily refresh; real-time screening on onboarding and payments.
  - Record Retention: Sentinel Archive WORM storage per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival; VaultEdge for working docs.
  - Privacy/Consent: GDPR/CCPA flags in Nexus360; consent ledger in AuroraLake.
  - Suitability: Product risk rating engine integrated to Nexus360; questionnaire in SignBridge.
- Limitations:
  - No onboarding of comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions listed in NFG-SAN-0042.
  - No omnibus accounts without look-through at 25% or control prong.
  - Digital-only onboarding restricted for Tier 3 clients; requires in-person verification.
- Dependencies:
  - E-signature via SignBridge for attestations.
  - Trade activation hinges on sanctions clearance and record archival confirmation.

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow
- Initiation (SLA: 2 hours to acknowledge)
  1. Create Quorum DD case type “COM-EXC” with Nexus360 Opportunity ID.
  2. Attach artifacts: UBO form (NFG-KYC-0110-F1), Source of Funds letter, ID/registration docs.
  3. Select exception category: Fee, Timeline, Sanctions false positive, EDD scope, Retention.
- Review (SLA: 8 business hours for decision)
  4. Compliance screens in AtlasScreen; logs evidence in case.
  5. Legal reviews regulatory exposure where needed.
  6. Sales Manager signs off on commercial impact.
- Decision and Communication
  7. Approval recorded in Quorum DD; e-sign via SignBridge for client acknowledgments.
  8. Nexus360 record updated; ServiceGate task auto-created for operations.
- Escalation Path
  - If SLA breach imminent, ping #deal-desk in Teams and escalate to L2 (Regional Compliance Manager). If unresolved within 4 hours, escalate to CCO.

Required Artifacts (no exceptions):
- Government ID (individual) or Articles of Incorporation (entity).
- UBO Declaration and Control Prong identification.
- Sanctions screening report (AtlasScreen) with timestamp.
- Suitability questionnaire (for complex products).
- Consent capture proof (SignBridge audit trail).
- Retention classification tag in Nexus360.

# 10. Procedures
Onboarding (Individuals)
1. Create Nexus360 Lead; capture consent via SignBridge. Control check: Consent_Privacy__c not null. Evidence: SignBridge audit ID.
2. Submit ID docs via SecureShare. Control check: Liveness/face match ≥0.85 in Veriscope. Evidence: KYC report PDF in VaultEdge.
3. Run sanctions/PEP. Control check: Sanctions_Status__c=Clear. Evidence: AtlasScreen report ID.
4. Suitability assessment for complex products. Control check: Suitability_Risk_Rating__c not High for restricted products. Evidence: Questionnaire file.
5. Archive all onboarding comms. Control check: Sentinel status WORM=LOCKED. Evidence: SEN-ID in Nexus360.

Onboarding (Entities)
1. Collect Articles, EIN, ownership chart, UBO form. Control check: UBO_Disclosed__c=true. Evidence: UBO form signed.
2. Verify control prong if no UBO >=25%. Control check: Control_Prongs__c populated. Evidence: Board resolution.
3. Sanctions/PEP on entity and UBOs. Control check: All statuses Clear or EDD approved. Evidence: AtlasScreen IDs.
4. Source-of-funds letter and expected activity JSON. Control check: JSON schema pass. Evidence: Attached JSON.
5. FI only: Wolfsberg Questionnaire. Control check: Completed within last 12 months. Evidence: Dated PDF.

Periodic Review
1. System generates due list weekly. Control check: Next_due within 30 days triggers task. Evidence: ServiceGate queue.
2. Refresh sanctions, update PEP changes, re-validate activity thresholds. Control check: No material deviation >30% without EDD. Evidence: HawkEye TM report.

Sanctions Hit Handling
1. Quorum DD case auto-created. Control check: L2 assignment within 2 hours. Evidence: Case timestamp.
2. Perform name resolution, secondary identifiers, adverse media. Control check: Documented rationale. Evidence: Case notes.
3. Decision: Clear (false positive), Reject, or Proceed with license (Legal). Control check: L3 approval if license needed.

Data Subject Request (DSR)
1. Log request in ServiceGate “PRIV-DSR”. Control check: Verify identity. Evidence: ID confirmation.
2. Retrieve data from AuroraLake using subject key. Control check: Fulfill within 30 days (EU/UK); 45 days (US). Evidence: Delivery receipt.
3. Update consent if requested. Control check: Consent ledger updated. Evidence: Nexus360 field updated.

Incident Response (Data/Compliance)
- RTO 4 hours (Sentinel); RPO 0 for archival data.
- Escalate via “INC-COM” in ServiceGate; notify CCO within 1 hour for material incidents.

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix
- “Why do you need my personal information?”
  - Response: Regulations require us to verify identity and monitor for financial crime. We collect only what’s necessary and secure it per industry standards. We cannot open accounts without this.
- “I don’t want to disclose owners.”
  - Response: Laws mandate beneficial ownership disclosure of 25%+ or control prong. We can provide our confidentiality terms; without it, we cannot proceed.
- “We were flagged on sanctions; this must be a mistake.”
  - Response: Many hits are name matches. We’ll resolve using additional identifiers within our 8-business-hour SLA before making a decision.
- “Can you waive the EDD fee?”
  - Response: Limited waivers exist. I can submit a Deal Desk request; Compliance must approve any EDD fee reduction.
- “Delete all my data now.”
  - Response: Some records must be retained for legal obligations (e.g., 5–7 years). We can restrict processing and delete what’s not legally required.

# 14. Regional Variations
- US
  - Regulations: BSA/AML, OFAC, SEC 17a-4 for broker-dealer records, Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI).
  - Retention: 6 years (customer records), WORM for electronic broker-dealer records.
- Canada
  - Regulations: PCMLTFA (FINTRAC), PIPEDA, OSFI Guideline B-8.
  - Retention: 5 years KYC records, DSR 30 days typical.
- EU/EEA
  - Regulations: AMLD5/6, GDPR. Consent opt-in required for marketing; DSR 30 days.
  - Retention: MiFID II 5 years (7 years if requested by NCAs) for recordings.
- UK
  - Regulations: UK Money Laundering Regulations, FCA COBS, UK GDPR.
  - Retention: 5 years MiFID voice/e-comm.
- Singapore/Hong Kong
  - Regulations: MAS Notice 626 (SG), AMLO (HK), PDPA (SG), PDPO (HK).
  - Retention: 5–7 years per product; cross-border data transfer controls.

Note: Where country rules conflict, apply the stricter control by default. See also: NFG-DATA-PRIV-0201 and NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.
