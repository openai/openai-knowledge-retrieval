# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)

- Who this is for: Advisors, RMs, Underwriters, and Support selling/servicing NFG life and health protection.
- What we offer:
  - INS-PTL: Personal Term Life (10/20/30-year)
  - INS-WL: Whole Life with cash value
  - INS-DI: Disability Income (own-occupation)
  - INS-CI: Critical Illness (lump-sum)
  - INS-KP: Key Person (business)
- Core SLAs:
  - Instant quotes ≤3s API; 95th percentile ≤5s.
  - Underwriting decision: Simplified ≤1 business day; Full UW ≤5 business days.
  - Policy issuance after acceptance: ≤2 business days.
  - Claims first response: ≤1 business day; resolution target ≤10 business days (non-contested).
- Do:
  - Capture income, smoking status, and beneficiary data in Atlas CRM before quote submission.
  - Use pricing guardrails; escalate discounts beyond thresholds.
  - Provide mandated disclosures (see Section 7).
- Don’t:
  - Promise coverage or rates prior to UW decision.
  - Email PHI without Helios DMS secure link.
- Escalations: Deal Desk via Slack #deal-desk-insurance and ticket DD-INS within 2 hours response; underwriting escalations to UW Duty Officer.

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases

- Individuals (US/CA/UK/EU)
  - Young families seeking low-cost protection (INS-PTL 20/30).
  - High earners seeking estate planning (INS-WL).
  - Professionals requiring income protection (INS-DI).
- SMEs (10–250 employees)
  - Protect against loss of key revenue drivers (INS-KP).
  - Offer voluntary benefits (INS-CI group add-on).
- Financial sponsor-backed founders
  - Collateral assignment for loans; layering INS-PTL and INS-KP.
- Eligibility Fit
  - Ages 18–70 (product-specific caps).
  - Stable income documentation for INS-DI.
  - Businesses operating ≥2 years for INS-KP.
- Non-Fit
  - High-risk occupations for certain products (e.g., offshore rig workers for DI).
  - Residents in embargoed countries (NFG-AML-1005).
  - Applicants with recent (≤12 months) major diagnoses for CI.

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions

- Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline (BANT)
  - Budget: “What monthly premium range are you comfortable with?” (target ratio ≤2% of net income)
  - Authority: “Who else will sign the application/consent?”
  - Need: “Primary purpose—income replacement, estate planning, business continuity?”
  - Timeline: “Do you need coverage effective by a specific date (e.g., loan closing)?”
- Health and Lifestyle (do not diagnose)
  - “Tobacco use in last 12 months?”
  - “Family history of early cardiac/cancer deaths?”
  - “Hazardous hobbies (aviation, diving, motorsports)?”
- Financial
  - “Annual income and liabilities?” (for DI benefit sizing)
  - “Business revenue trend last 2 years?” (INS-KP)
- Red Flags
  - Mismatch between requested face amount and insurable interest.
  - Multiple concurrent applications at different carriers.
  - Incomplete identity docs (KYC failure risk).

# 4. Product/Policy Overview

- INS-PTL (Personal Term Life)
  - Terms: 10/20/30 years; face amount $100,000–$5,000,000.
  - Riders: Accelerated Death Benefit (ADB), Waiver of Premium (WOP).
  - Limitations: Rates step-up on renewal; conversion to INS-WL available before final 5 years.
- INS-WL (Whole Life)
  - Permanent coverage; guaranteed cash value with participating dividends (non-guaranteed).
  - Face amount $50,000–$3,000,000; policy loans up to 90% cash value, APR 6.00%.
- INS-DI (Disability Income)
  - Benefit: 60% of gross monthly income up to $15,000/month, elimination 60/90/180 days.
  - Own-occupation definition for first 24 months; then reasonable occupation.
  - Exclusions: Pre-existing conditions lookback 12/12.
- INS-CI (Critical Illness)
  - Lump sum $25,000–$500,000 for covered conditions (cancer, MI, stroke).
  - 30-day survival period; one-time payout.
- INS-KP (Key Person)
  - For SMEs to cover loss of key executives; face $250,000–$10,000,000.
  - Requires financial justification (10x–15x key person’s contribution).
- Dependencies
  - Aegis PAS for issuance, Mercury Billing for premium, Helios DMS for e-sign, Atlas CRM for prospecting.
  - MIB and Rx checks via Hermes ESB; medical exams via Medix partner.

# 5. Pricing, Fees, and Discounting

Pricing is risk-based: age, sex, smoking, health class (Preferred Plus, Preferred, Standard, Substandard A–D), and riders.

Pricing Table (illustrative, US region, monthly premiums)

- INS-PTL 20-year, $500,000 face:
  - Male, 35, Preferred: $28.30; Standard: $41.75
  - Female, 35, Preferred: $24.10; Standard: $35.20
- INS-WL $250,000 face:
  - Male, 45, Standard: $315.50; Preferred: $287.90 (dividends not guaranteed)
- INS-DI $8,000/month benefit, 90-day elimination:
  - Age 40, White-collar: $126.40; Blue-collar: $182.75
- Fees
  - Policy fee: $4.00/month (all products)
  - Rider fees: WOP +$0.10 per $1,000 face; ADB included.

Discounting Guardrails

- Standard advisor discretionary discount (D-1): up to 5% first-year premium (not fees).
- Strategic deal discount (D-2): 6–12% with Deal Desk approval.
- Enterprise partner program (D-3): up to 15% for group/affinity ≥100 members; requires VP Sales + Compliance approval.
- Prohibited: Discounting beyond 15%, or differential pricing based on prohibited characteristics (NFG-FAIR-0102).

Approval Matrix

- Level L1: Up to 5% — Advisor + RM self-approval; log in Atlas CRM DiscountReason.
- Level L2: >5% to 12% — Deal Desk Manager (DDM) approval; ticket DD-INS; SLA 8 business hours.
- Level L3: >12% to 15% — VP Sales + Compliance Officer sign-off; SLA 2 business days.
- Level L4: Any exception to underwriting class or fee waiver — Chief Underwriter + Legal; SLA 3 business days.

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow

Steps

1) Qualify in Atlas CRM
- Create Opportunity with Stage=“Discovery”, Segment=Individual/SME, ProductCode(s).
- Required artifacts: KYC docs, Needs Analysis form NA-INS-2025, preliminary quote PDF.

2) Submit Discount Request (if any)
- Create DD-INS ticket via Portal; attach quote, rationale, competitive intel.
- SLA: initial response ≤2 business hours; resolution per Approval Matrix.

3) Underwriting Intake
- Trigger UW via Atlas “Submit to UW” button; pushes to Aegis PAS with applicant data.
- Control check: Automated KYC (NFG-KYC-0110) and AML screening via Hermes.

4) Decision and Offer
- UW classification returned to Atlas; generate Term Sheet (Section 12).
- Evidence: UW decision ID UW-DEC-2025-#### stored in Helios DMS.

5) Client Acceptance & E-sign
- Send e-sign via Helios; collect ACH/CC for initial premium.
- Control: Payment tokenization PCI-SEG-004; no card data in CRM notes.

6) Issue Policy
- Aegis PAS issues; Mercury Billing enrolls auto-pay; policy number POL-YYYY-#######.
- SLA: issuance ≤2 business days post-acceptance.

7) Handoff to Support
- Create Service Record SRV-INS-####; schedule 30/180-day check-ins.

Escalation Path

- Pricing stalemate: DDM → VP Sales.
- UW delay > SLA: UW Duty Officer (pager x7777), then Chief Underwriter.
- System outage: Janus API Incident Bridge; RTO 2 hours, RPO 15 minutes; comms via StatusPage.

# 7. Risk and Compliance Disclosures

- Mandatory Statements (verbatim)
  - “Premiums and benefits are subject to underwriting approval. Rates are not guaranteed until policy issuance.”
  - “Dividends for participating whole life are not guaranteed.”
  - “This is a solicitation of insurance. A licensed agent may contact you.”
  - “We collect and process personal data in accordance with NFG Privacy Policy and GDPR where applicable.”
- Suitability and Replacement
  - Complete Suitability checklist SUIT-CK-0201 for replacements; provide state-specific forms (US).
- Recordkeeping
  - Store all sales communications in Helios DMS per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival (7-year minimum).
- AML/KYC
  - ID verification prior to binding; screen against sanctions lists (OFAC, HMT).

  - DealDeskTicketID (string) e.g., DD-INS-001234
- Applicant
  - ApplicantID (string) e.g., APP-2025-0001
  - FirstName/LastName (string)
  - DOB (date, must be 18–70) e.g., 1987-06-12
  - SSN/NIN (masked; store last4 only) e.g., ***-**-1234
  - SmokingStatus (enum: Non, Occasional, Daily)
  - OccupationCode (NAICS/SOC mapping)
  - AnnualIncome (currency) e.g., $165,000.00
  - BeneficiaryType (enum: Individual, Trust, Business)
  - HealthClass (enum; post-UW) e.g., Preferred
- Policy
  - PolicyNumber (string) e.g., POL-2025-0012345
  - FaceAmount (currency) e.g., $500,000.00
  - TermLength (integer, years) e.g., 20
  - Riders (array) e.g., ["ADB","WOP"]
  - PremiumMonthly (currency) e.g., $28.30
Validation Rules

- FaceAmount must not exceed 20x annual income for INS-PTL unless UW override.
- INS-DI benefit ≤60% income; hard stop in Janus API.
- PII fields encrypted at rest (AES-256); no full SSN in free-text fields.

SLOs and Error Patterns

- SLO: 99.9% Quote API availability; max latency p95 500ms internal.
- Common errors:
  - 422 POLICY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED — FaceAmount>20x income (INS-PTL).
  - 409 DUPLICATE_CASE — existing open UW case for applicant.
  - 403 CONSENT_MISSING — MIB/Rx consent absent.

# 10. Procedures

Onboarding (New Policy)

1) Discovery and Needs Analysis
- Complete NA-INS-2025; store in Helios DMS.
- Control: Suitability SUIT-CK-0201 must be attached before UW submission.

2) Quote and Present
- Generate quote via Atlas → Janus API; present 2–3 options.
- Evidence: Quote IDs logged in CRM.

3) UW Submission
- Collect e-consents; submit case; schedule medical if required (Medix).
- Control: Hermes ESB confirms MIB/Rx pull success.

4) Offer and Acceptance
- Send Term Sheet; discuss riders and billing options.
- Evidence: Client acceptance e-sign; payment token created in Mercury.

5) Issue and Welcome
- Verify policy data matches Term Sheet; issue and send welcome kit.
- Control: Two-person verification in Aegis PAS for face amount ≥$1,000,000.

Servicing (Endorsements/Claims)

1) Endorsements
- Create SRV-INS; update beneficiary or address; require identity verification.
- Control: Changes above risk thresholds (beneficiary change to non-relative) flagged to Compliance.

2) Claims Intake
- Record Claim INT-CLM-####; collect death certificate/physician statement.
- SLA: first response ≤1 business day; status updates every 3 days.
- Control: Fraud screening via Orion patterns.

Exceptions

- UW Class Override
  - Submit EXC-UW form; justify with labs/APS; L4 approval.
- Backdate Requests
  - Allowed ≤60 days to save age; document client acknowledgment; DDM approval.

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix

- “Premiums are too high.”
  - Response: “We can adjust face amount or term to meet your budget. For example, moving from $750k to $500k reduces monthly by ~30%. Discounts up to 5% are available today.”
- “I’m healthy—why need CI/DI?”
  - Response: “CI/DI protect income and expenses during unexpected illness or injury. 1 in 4 workers will experience a disability before retirement. Benefits pay regardless of employment.”
- “I don’t want a medical exam.”
  - Response: “Many applicants qualify for simplified underwriting up to $1,000,000 with no exam, based on MIB/Rx checks.”
- “Whole life is a bad investment.”
  - Response: “WL is first an insurance product with guaranteed coverage and cash values. Dividends are not guaranteed; we can model conservative scenarios and compare to term + invest-the-difference.”
- “Key person seems excessive.”
  - Response: “Coverage is tied to financial impact. We use documented contribution (10–15x) to right-size the policy.”

# 14. Regional Variations

- US
  - Replacement forms required in most states; NAIC Model Replacement Reg.
  - CI definitions aligned with state mandates; some riders unavailable in CA.
- Canada (CA)
  - LICAT capital impacts for WL; provincial privacy (PIPA/Quebec Law 25).
  - Smoking definition includes vaping; 24-month lookback.
- UK
  - FCA ICOBS requires demands-and-needs statement; cooling-off 30 days.
  - Premium tax (IPT) applies; reflected in Mercury Billing calculations.
- EU
  - GDPR consent granularity; data minimization enforced (store last4 only).
  - Cross-border selling requires passporting checks; consult Compliance.
- APAC-SG
  - MAS guidelines; fact-find form FF-SG-INS mandatory; cooling-off 14 days.
