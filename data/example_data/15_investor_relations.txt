# Quick Reference (TL;DR)
- Who: Institutional investors, rating agencies, sell-side analysts, proxy advisors. Route retail to Support (RET-CARE-010).
- What: Schedule management access, share public materials, handle inquiries, manage data subscriptions, coordinate roadshows, and respond to shareholder concerns.
- When: Initial triage in 4 business hours; meeting scheduling within 2 business days; documentation within 1 business day of events.
- Where: Log all interactions in NFG-SFDC (Record Type=Investor); attach artifacts to IRIS; archive via ArchiveVault per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.
- SLAs: Response SLA 4 hours (P2), 1 hour (P1—earnings week); Meeting SLA 2 business days; Data Request SLA 3 business days; RPO 15 minutes; RTO 2 hours for IRIS and CommsHub.
- Do:
  - Use only public information unless under approved NDA and inside-wall controls (NFG-IB-032).
  - Read “Required Statements” before calls; use CommsHub templates.
  - Escalate suspected MNPI exposure within 15 minutes.
- Don’t:
  - Provide forward-looking guidance beyond public disclosures.
  - Offer research or inducements violating MiFID II.
  - Promise preferential access without compliance approval.

# Customer Personas and Use Cases
- Institutional Equity Holders (Asset Managers, Pension Funds)
  - Use cases: Quarterly updates; governance queries; ESG disclosures; voting intent dialogue.
  - Fit: Holdings >$10M in NFG-ETF-CORE or NFG-PRF-A; active stewardship teams.
- Debt Investors (Sovereign Wealth, Insurance)
  - Use cases: MTN program updates; covenant clarifications; ratings review support; roadshow meetings.
  - Fit: Prior participation in NFG-MTN tranches; appetite >$25M.
- Sell-Side Analysts
  - Use cases: Model clarifications; segment metrics; earnings call follow-ups.
  - Fit: Coverage list maintained in IRIS; require equal-access protocols.
- Proxy Advisors
  - Use cases: Governance, compensation policy discussions pre-AGM.
  - Fit: Recognized firms; discussion limited to public facts.
- Non-Fit/Redirect
  - Retail investors: Route to Retail Support.
  - Media: Route to Corporate Communications (NFG-COMMS-0044).
  - Vendors soliciting IR tools: Route to Procurement.

# Qualification and Discovery Questions
BANT/CHAMP prompts:
- Budget: “Are you currently allocating funds to additional NFG securities or data services? Range?”
- Authority: “Will you be the attendee(s) for management meetings? Any compliance constraints for your firm?”
- Need: “Which KPIs or disclosures are critical to your thesis? Debt vs equity focus?”
- Timing: “Is this tied to an upcoming IC date, rating committee, or AGM?”
- Metrics: “Which segments require deeper detail? E.g., ETF net flows, MTN tenor mix.”
- Red Flags:
  - Requests for non-public KPIs or intra-quarter guidance.
  - Offering to share competitor MNPI.
  - Quid pro quo for research, access, or commissions.
  - Requests to change published disclosures without governance review.

# Product/Policy Overview
- Public Information Access
  - Channels: IR website, EDGAR/SEDAR filings, earnings call webcasts, investor deck.
  - Policy: Equal access (NFG-IR-Policy-100), Reg FD adherence.
- Investor Access Program (IAP)
  - Tiers: Basic (public distribution, no fee), Plus (priority Q&A routing), Premium (concierge scheduling, bespoke analyst days under equal-access constraints).
  - Constraints: No preferential disclosure; access governed by public availability.
- Bespoke Data Subscription (NFG-IR-DATA-CUST)
  - Deliverables: Aggregated, anonymized metrics (e.g., product-level AUM lagged T+15 days).
  - Controls: No client-level data; k-anonymity >= 10; latency >= T+10 to avoid MNPI.
- Roadshows and Non-Deal Roadshows (NDRs)
  - Coverage: MTN issuances, strategic updates.
  - Materials: Pre-cleared slides (Legal-CM); Q&A scripts.
- Information Barriers
  - Inside vs outside wall, codified in NFG-IB-032. All IR operates outside wall. Any potential crossing requires Compliance case and wall-cross memo.

# Pricing, Fees, and Discounting
Investor Access Program pricing (where allowed by regulation; see Regional Variations):
- Basic (NFG-IR-ACCESS-B): $0.00/year; distribution of public releases; standard Q&A queue.
- Plus (NFG-IR-ACCESS-P): $15,000.00/year; priority queue; quarterly small-group Q&A; invite-only public events.
- Premium (NFG-IR-ACCESS-PREM): $45,000.00/year; dedicated scheduling support; guaranteed seats in public events; limited bespoke scheduling windows.

Bespoke Data Subscription:
- Standard Package (Monthly, T+15): $60,000.00/year.
- Enhanced Package (Biweekly, T+15): $90,000.00/year.
- Custom Fields Add-on: $5,000.00/year per additional approved metric.

Discounting Guardrails:
- Max discretionary discount:
  - IR Director up to 10%.
  - VP-IR up to 20%.
  - CCO + CFO joint approval required >20% or any free arrangement.
- Multi-year prepay: Additional 5% discount with CFO approval.

Approval Matrix
- Level 0: Basic Program — No approval; auto-enroll; RevOps provisioning.
- Level 1: Plus/Premium <=10% discount — IR Director; RevOps ticket.
- Level 2: Data Subscription or >10%–20% discount — VP-IR; Legal-CM review; Compliance attestation (MiFID II inducements).
- Level 3: Any custom metric; >20% discount; NDA with wall-cross — CCO + CFO + Legal-CM sign-off; InfoSec DPA review.

# Deal Desk Workflow
1. Intake (SLA: 4 business hours)
   - Log lead in NFG-SFDC (Record Type=Investor; Lead Source=IR-Web/Analyst/Conference).
   - Attach inbound request email to IRIS; tag “IAP” or “DATA”.
   - Auto-ack via CommsHub template IR-ACK-001.
2. Qualification (SLA: same day)
   - Validate persona fit and region. Run MiFID II inducement check via ComplianceBot.
   - If Data Subscription: run k-anonymity pre-check and metrics catalog match.
3. Structuring (SLA: 2 business days)
   - Select tier/pricing; prepare Term Sheet (see template).
   - For custom metrics: open Legal-CM JIRA LEG-IR-####; attach metric definitions.
4. Approval (SLA: 1 business day)
   - Route per Approval Matrix using Deal Desk workflow in NFG-SFDC (Path=IR-Deal).
   - Evidence: approvals stored in NFG-SFDC Related List “Approvals__c”; auto-archived.
5. Contracting (SLA: 2 business days)
   - Generate Order Form and DPA via DocGen; NDA if required (no MNPI access unless wall-crossed).
   - eSign through NFG-Auth integrated SignFlow; archive to ArchiveVault.
6. Provisioning (SLA: 1 business day)
   - RevOps creates access in IRIS; CommsHub adds to distribution lists; SSO enablement.
   - Validate against data latency constraints (>= T+10).
7. Post-Sale Handover
   - Assign CSM-IR owner; schedule onboarding call; set Success Plan in NFG-SFDC.
8. Escalation Path
   - P1 (earnings week, issuer event): IR Director -> VP-IR -> CCO.
   - P2 (standard): Deal Desk Manager -> VP-IR within 8 hours.
   - Legal issues: route to Legal-CM hotline LEG-HOT-24x7.

# Risk and Compliance Disclosures
Required statements (read or included in written communications):
- Equal Access: “We can only discuss information already publicly disclosed by Northbridge Financial Group.”
- Forward-Looking: “Any statements about future performance are subject to risks and uncertainties; please refer to our latest filings.”
- No MNPI: “We cannot comment on intra-quarter performance beyond what is public.”
- Recording & Archival: “This call may be recorded and archived for compliance per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.”
- MiFID II Inducements: “Access services do not include research and are not contingent on brokerage commissions.”
- UK/EU MAR: “We do not disclose inside information outside the normal course of public disclosure.”

Control checks:
- Use CommsHub templates with disclosure blocks.
- Archive all emails/call recordings to ArchiveVault within 24 hours.
- Quarterly audit by GRC on sample of interactions (NFG-GRC-IR-AUD-22).

# Procedures
A. Onboard an Institutional Investor to IAP Plus
1. Intake: Create Investor__c; set IAP_Tier__c=Plus; validate Region__c and Persona__c.
2. Compliance check: Run inducements check; log result in Compliance_Notes__c.
3. Order Form: Generate via DocGen template IR-IAP-OF-Plus; include fee and term dates.
4. Signatures: Route through SignFlow; verify KYC if billing entity is new vendor (NFG-KYC-0110).
5. Provisioning: RevOps provisions IRIS access; ensure Consent_Marketing__c captured.
6. Control check: Confirm archive of contract in ArchiveVault; attach archive_ref on record.
7. Evidence: Upload provisioning confirmation from IRIS to SFDC under “Files”.

B. Schedule Management Meeting (Debt Investor)
1. Verify persona=Debt and holdings in NFG-MTN >$25M (Holdings_Size__c).
2. Propose time slots via NFG-Events; ensure disclosure slide pack is attached.
3. Pre-brief: Send Management Q&A outline; include Required Statements.
4. Meeting: Record via CommsHub; tag IR-EVT; avoid non-public topics.
5. Post: Send written recap with public links; archive recording and recap within 24 hours.
6. Control check: Compliance spot-review flag set=true; GRC sampling list updated.

C. Fulfill Bespoke Data Subscription Request
1. Qualification: Confirm metrics requested exist in Approved Metrics Catalog (IRIS: cat_id IR-MET-2025).
2. Privacy check: Validate k-anonymity >=10 and latency >= T+10; document in LEG-IR ticket.
3. Deal Desk: Prepare Term Sheet; route approvals per Level 2/3 as applicable.
4. Build: Data Engineering enables feed; restrict to investor_id via tokenized S3 link.
5. Delivery: CommsHub notification with links and Required Statements.
6. Control check: Automated test delivers masked sample; QA sign-off attached.
7. Evidence: ArchiveVault references for contract, approvals, QA report.

D. Exception — Suspected MNPI Exposure
1. Stop conversation immediately; state: “We cannot discuss non-public information.”
2. Escalate within 15 minutes via Compliance Hotline (COMP-24x7) and file IR-INC ticket.
3. Tag all artifacts “MNPI-SUSPECT”; restrict access to inside-wall group.
4. Compliance determines wall-cross necessity; if approved, issue wall-cross memo with expiry.
5. Control check: IRIS case shows lock icon; audit trail immutable per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.
6. Resume only with Compliance clearance; send written confirmation to investor.

# Objection Handling Matrix
- “We need intra-quarter numbers.”
  - Response: “We can’t provide intra-quarter figures beyond public disclosures. We can walk through the drivers we’ve published and timing of next release.”
- “Competitor X shared more detail.”
  - Response: “We adhere to equal-access and regulatory standards. Here’s what we’ve disclosed, and we’ll log your request for future disclosure review.”
- “Fees look high for data.”
  - Response: “Our pricing reflects compliance-safe aggregation, latency controls, and QA. For multi-year prepay we can consider up to 5% additional discount with CFO approval.”
- “Prefer one-on-one with the CEO.”
  - Response: “We can arrange subject to availability, ensuring discussions remain within public information. We’ll also provide materials ahead for efficient use of time.”
- “Is this research under MiFID II?”
  - Response: “No. Investor Access services exclude research and are not contingent on brokerage services. We can provide public materials and access scheduling support.”

# Regional Variations
- US (Reg FD): No selective disclosure; investor access fees allowed if non-research and not tied to brokerage.
- UK/EU (MAR; MiFID II):
  - Inducements: Do not bundle access with execution; separate invoices; obtain annual inducement attestation.
  - Research Unbundling: Label all materials “Corporate Access—Non-Research.”
- Canada (NI 51-102): Timely disclosure standards; SEDAR+ filings synchronized; bilingual (EN/FR) support for Quebec institutional investors.
- APAC: Jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction marketing restrictions; avoid performance comparisons not in local factsheets; route to Regional Compliance for Japan and Australia queries.
