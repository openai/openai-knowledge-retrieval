# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)

- Who: Small/Mid-Market businesses (1–500 FTEs), annual revenue $250,000–$250,000,000; supported entities: LLC, Corp, LLP, Nonprofit (US/EU), Sole Proprietor (region-specific).
- What: Business Checking, Money Market, Treasury (ACH/Wires/Lockbox), Remote Deposit Capture (RDC), Merchant Services, and Working Capital Line of Credit (LOC) adjunct.
- When: Standard onboarding SLA 3 business days (T+3) from complete KYC package; expedited path 1 business day with pre-approval flags.
- Where: Orion CRM drives workflow; Atlas Core Banking for account provisioning; Helios for payments; KYC Sentinel for risk; DocuSafe for records (retention 7 yrs per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival).
- Do:
  - Use Qualification matrix in Section 3.
  - Capture mandatory CRM fields (Section 8) before submitting to Deal Desk.
  - Price within guardrails (Section 5) or route approvals via Nova.
  - Disclose fees and transaction limits verbatim (Section 7).
- Don’t:
  - Promise same-day onboarding without KYC completion.
  - Waive monthly fees beyond your approval band.
  - Onboard entities in sanctioned jurisdictions (see Section 14).
- SLAs:
  - KYC review: 24 hours (US/CA), 48 hours (EU/UK).
  - Account opening: 4 hours after KYC pass.
  - Payment rail enablement: ACH 24 hours; Wires 4 hours post-Nostro test.
- Escalations: Deal Desk via Slack #deal-desk-bb (P1 in 2 hours), or ServiceNow INC-BB severity matrix (Section 6).

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases

- SMB Owner-Operator (10–50 FTEs; $1–$10M revenue)
  - Use cases: Basic checking, ACH payroll, card settlement, RDC.
  - Fit: Limited international activity; <5,000 monthly transactions.
- Mid-Market Controller (50–300 FTEs; $10–$150M revenue)
  - Use cases: Multicurrency wires, positive pay, dual approvals.
  - Fit: Needs Treasury entitlements and ERP integration.
- E-commerce Scale-up (5–100 FTEs; $2–$50M revenue)
  - Use cases: High-volume ACH/merchant settlements, API access.
  - Fit: >50,000 monthly transactions, needs Helios API and fraud monitoring.
- Nonprofit Finance Director
  - Use cases: Fee concessions, donation batch processing, grant segregation.
  - Fit: Eligibility for nonprofit pricing tier with board minutes and EIN/charity number.

Eligibility Fit:
- Yes if: Registered business, verifiable ownership ≤4 UBOs >25%, clean sanctions screen, anticipated monthly average ledger ≥$5,000.
- No if: MSB without enhanced due diligence (EDD), crypto exchanges, cannabis (US federal charter), sanctioned countries, shell/blank-check companies, anonymous trusts.

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions

BANT/CHAMP Prompts:
- Budget: What monthly fee budget and current bank fees do you have? Any fee waivers tied to balances?
- Authority: Who signs on accounts? Dual signers needed? Treasurer/Controller involvement?
- Need: What volumes for ACH, wires, RDC? Cash handling needs? International currency needs?
- Timing: Target go-live date? Payroll cycles? Any critical payments in next 10 days?
- Metrics: Current payment failure rate, fraud incidents, average DSO.
- Pain: Limits with current bank (e.g., low ACH limits, slow onboarding)?
- Constraints: ERP/TMS integration? SSO requirements? Browser/network constraints?

Red Flags:
- Refusal to disclose UBOs >10%.
- High chargeback rates (>1%) without mitigation plan.
- Unclear source of funds or shell entity structures.
- Requests to bypass dual control for wires.

# 4. Product/Policy Overview

Capabilities:
- Business Checking (BB-CHK-100): Non-interest; 300 free items/mo; FDIC/FSCS equivalent coverage per region.
- Money Market (BB-MM-120): Tiered interest; six preauthorized transfers/day limit per regional rules.
- Treasury Suite (BB-TM-200): ACH origination, wires (domestic/international), positive pay, lockbox, controlled disbursement.
- RDC (BB-RDC-220): Desktop and mobile; daily limit configurable; check image quality scoring via Helios ML.
- ACH (BB-ACH-240): Same Day ACH available; cutoffs 16:00 ET (US).
- Wires (BB-WIRE-260): Domestic cutoff 17:00 local; SWIFT for intl; MT103 confirmation.
- Merchant Services (BB-MRS-280): Card settlement via partner gateway; next-day funding.
- Working Capital LOC (BB-LOC-300): Optional; separate credit approval; cross-sell discount bundle.

Limitations:
- No cash vault services in APAC.
- No onboarding of virtual asset service providers (VASPs) in US per NFG-COM-AML-052.
- Max daily wire limit default $250,000; higher requires risk approval.
- API access limited to OAuth2 service accounts; no personal tokens.

Dependencies:
- KYC Sentinel must return “Low/Moderate” risk; “High” requires EDD and VP Risk sign-off.
- Treasury services require dual control configured in Helios Admin.
- SSO via SAML2 with SCIM provisioning for enterprises >50 seats.

# 5. Pricing, Fees, and Discounting

Pricing Table (USD; regional FX equivalents apply):

- BB-CHK-100: $25.00 monthly; waiver at $10,000 avg balance; $0.40 per excess item >300.
- BB-MM-120: $35.00 monthly; interest tiers: 0.75% APY (≤$250k), 1.10% APY ($250k–$2M).
- BB-ACH-240: $15.00 monthly; $0.12 per transaction; Same Day +$0.055/txn.
- BB-WIRE-260: Domestic $18.00 outgoing/$10.00 incoming; Intl $35.00 outgoing.
- BB-RDC-220: $45.00 monthly/device; $0.05 per check; daily limit default $50,000.
- Positive Pay: $30.00 monthly; $0.02/item review.
- Merchant Services: Interchange+ 25 bps and $0.08/txn; PCI non-compliance $19.95/mo.

Approval Matrix (Discounting and Limits):

- Level 0 (Advisor): Up to 10% fee discount; waive 1 month fees for service outage; increase wire limit to $500,000 with documented need; evidence in Orion.
- Level 1 (RM Manager): Up to 20% discount; custom ACH pricing to $0.08/txn; wire limit to $1,000,000; require Nova price justification ID.
- Level 2 (Regional Director): Up to 35% discount; bundle waiver up to 3 months; wire limit to $5,000,000; EDD proof; Risk consult ticket.
- Level 3 (VP, Commercial): Exceptions beyond 35%; bespoke tiering; pricing floors cannot go below variable cost + 20 bps per NFG-PRC-002.

Guardrails:
- Nonprofit concession: waive monthly maintenance; per-item fees unchanged.
- Term ≥12 months for discounts >20%.
- Margin tests run via Nova; attach Nova-PRC-Ref (e.g., NOVA-2025-11345).

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow

Steps (target SLA end-to-end 48 hours after complete package):

1) Qualify
- Capture discovery details; set Opportunity Stage = “Qualified” in Orion (opp_type: BUSINESS_BANKING).
- Control check: Red flags cleared.

2) Build Pricing
- Use Nova Pricing Engine; select product_codes; apply discounts within level; generate quote ID (e.g., QUO-2025-00456).
- Control check: Margin ≥ minimum per NFG-PRC-002; Nova report attached to Opportunity.

3) KYC Package
- Initiate via Orion “Start KYC” button; KYC Sentinel workflow: collect BOI (UBOs >25%), IDs, EIN/Registration.
- Control check: Argus Sanctions screen “Clear”; risk_score ≤60.

4) Deal Desk Submission
- Submit form DEAL-BB in Orion; required artifacts: Nova quote, KYC summary, term sheet draft, implementation plan.
- SLA: Deal Desk triage 4 hours; decision 24 hours.

5) Underwriting (if LOC or limits >$1M)
- Route to Commercial Credit (Credit App ID APP-2025-0001); SLA 48 hours.
- Control check: Financials 2 years; DSCR ≥1.20.

6) Approval & Doc
- Receive DD-APP-Ref (e.g., DDAPP-2025-09021); generate term sheet; send via DocuSafe eSign.
- SLA: Doc execution within 2 business days.

7) Provisioning
- Atlas account creation within 4 hours of doc execution; Helios entitlements within 24 hours.
- Control check: Dual control enforced; limits implemented per approval.

8) Go-Live & Handoff
- Welcome call; training; support contacts shared.
- Evidence: Go-Live Checklist stored in DocuSafe (DSF-2025-33421).

Escalation Path:
- P1 (client at risk of churn): Notify Deal Desk Lead (Leo Hart) and VP Commercial; ServiceNow INC-BB-P1; ETA response 2 hours.
- P2: Manager approval; response 8 hours.
- P3: Standard; response next business day.

# 7. Risk and Compliance Disclosures

Required Statements:
- Account terms and fees are subject to change with 30 days’ notice per NFG-BB-T&C-2025.
- Funds availability follows Reg CC analogues; RDC deposits may be held up to 2 business days.
- International wires subject to correspondent fees; FX spreads disclosed at time of send.
- Data privacy complies with NFG-DATA-PR-100 (GDPR/CCPA equivalents).
- We do not bank prohibited industries listed in NFG-AML-PROH-041; enhanced due diligence may apply.
- ACH origination requires NACHA rule compliance; violations may result in suspension.

Recordkeeping:
- Communications and agreements archived for 7 years (NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival).
- Call notes and acknowledgments logged in Orion within 24 hours.

- sanctions_status (enum: Clear|Potential|Hit; readonly)
- dual_control_required (bool; default true)
- quote_id (string; required when stage >= Proposal; e.g., QUO-2025-00456)
- dd_app_ref (string; set post-approval; e.g., DDAPP-2025-09021)
- implementation_date (date; target go-live)
- primary_contact_id (lookup; required; e.g., CUST-12345)
- kyc_package_id (string; readonly; e.g., KYC-2025-77102)

Validation:
- If intl_activity = true, wire_monthly_volume must be >0.
- If discount >20%, approval_level must be ≥ Level 1 with approver_id present.
- risk_score >60 triggers edd_required = true and blocks stage advancement.

# 10. Procedures

A) Onboarding (New Business Checking + Treasury)
1. Discovery and Qualification
- Complete Section 3 prompts. Evidence: Discovery Note in Orion.
2. KYC Intake
- Send DocuSafe link; collect IDs, BOI, formation docs. Evidence: KYC-2025-XXXX.
- Control: Sanctions Clear; risk_score ≤60.
3. Pricing and Term Sheet
- Generate QUO in Nova; include all services; attach to Orion.
- Control: Discount within approval band; Nova margin test pass.
4. Deal Desk Approval
- Submit DEAL-BB; attach artifacts; track DDAPP ref.
5. Documentation and eSign
- Term sheet via DocuSafe; require two officer signatures if dual control.
6. Account and Services Provisioning
- Atlas account open; assign account_id; enable ACH/Wires in Helios with approved limits.
- Control: Dual control configured before enabling wires.
7. Training and Go-Live
- 30-minute session; share cutoffs; provide support contacts.
8. Post-Go-Live Review (T+14)
- Check usage vs. plan; adjust limits; log in Orion.

B) Servicing (Limit Increase Request)
1. Intake ticket in ServiceNow (SRV-BB-LIMIT); attach 3 months statements if external.
2. Risk review (ACH returns <0.5%, chargebacks <1%).
3. Approval per matrix; update Helios limits with DDAPP ref.
4. Confirm to client; update Orion notes.

C) Exceptions (High-Risk Entity but Strategic)
1. Flag in Orion as EXCEPTION_REQ = true; auto-route to Risk Committee.
2. Prepare EDD memo (ownership chart, source of funds, compliance posture).
3. VP Risk decision within 72 hours; if approved, set risk_controls = enhanced.
4. Controls: Lower limits initially; daily monitoring; 30-day review.

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix

- “Your monthly fees are higher than my current bank.”
  - Response: We can tailor fees based on your volumes. With your ACH volume at 12,000/month, Nova supports $0.09/txn and a checking fee waiver at $25,000 average balance. Let’s model total cost of ownership.
- “I need higher wire limits on day one.”
  - Response: We can provision $1,000,000 with manager approval today, and scale to $5,000,000 after 30 days of clean history. This protects you and meets NFG-AML-041 thresholds.
- “Onboarding takes too long.”
  - Response: Once we receive complete KYC documents, our SLA is T+3 business days; expedited is T+1 for low-risk profiles. I’ll help assemble your package today to hit the faster path.
- “I don’t want dual control.”
  - Response: Dual control is required for wires to mitigate fraud per NFG-FRD-PP-022. We can configure flexible approvers to keep your process fast and secure.
- “We need API access with no user approvals.”
  - Response: We support OAuth2 service accounts and role-based entitlements. For wires, an approval webhook is required to comply with payment control policy; ACH can be fully automated within approved limits.

# 14. Regional Variations

US:
- Same Day ACH per NACHA; cutoff 16:00 ET.
- Cannabis-related businesses excluded (federal charter).

Canada:
- EFT instead of ACH; pricing parity; cutoff 16:00 ET.
- FINTRAC KYC requirements; beneficial ownership threshold 25%.

UK/EU:
- SEPA Credit Transfer; cutoff 15:00 CET; Faster Payments for domestic UK.
- PSD2 SCA for portal access; GDPR data subject rights fulfilled via NFG-DATA-PR-100.

APAC (SG/HK):
- RTGS domestic rails; no cash vault support.
- Enhanced sanctions screening; higher false positive rate; SLA +24 hours KYC.

Sanctions/Restrictions:
- No onboarding from OFAC/EU consolidated lists; Argus enforces.
- Dual language docs for FR/DE clients; translated templates available.

See also: Treasury Management Deep Dive (NFG-TREASURY-0020) and Onboarding Playbook (NFG-ONB-0042).
