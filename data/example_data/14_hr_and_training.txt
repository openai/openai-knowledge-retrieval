# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)

- Who: Frontline advisors, sales, RMs, underwriters, support needing onboarding, certifications, or training access; managers approving budgets and exceptions.
- What: Enroll, certify, and track training in LearnHub; update certification status in ApexCRM automatically; request external training via ServiceGate.
- When:
  - Onboarding modules: within 10 business days of start date.
  - Mandatory annual compliance training: by 2025-12-15.
  - Product enablement: prior to pitching or binding policies.
- Where:
  - LearnHub: https://learnhub.nfg.com
  - Training Requests: ServiceGate > HR & Training > Training Request
  - Certification status: ApexCRM > User Profile > Certifications tab.
- SLAs:
  - Training enrollment requests triaged in 1 business day; fulfilled in 3 business days.
  - External training budget approval: 5 business days.
  - Certification sync to CRM: near-real-time (<15 minutes).
  - LMS incident RTO: 4 hours; RPO: 0 hours (active-active).
- Do:
  - Complete “NFG Sales Ethics (TRN-COMP-100)” before client meetings.
  - Use approved scripts for licensing disclosures.
  - Attach completion certificates to DocVault using the provided naming standard.
- Don’t:
  - Pitch restricted products without the product certification badge in ApexCRM.
  - Self-fund external training and seek reimbursement without pre-approval.
  - Share LMS credentials or bypass SSO.

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases

- Advisor (Retail/Wealth)
  - Needs: Licensing modules, product training, annual compliance.
  - Use cases: Pre-appointment prep, continuing education credits (CE), new product launch readiness.
  - Fit: Assigned to a client-facing cost center; active in ApexCRM.

- Relationship Manager (Institutional)
  - Needs: Complex product enablement, cross-border rules, confidentiality modules.
  - Use cases: RFP response prep, solutioning workshops, renewal cycles.
  - Fit: Handles accounts in multiple regions; requires MiFID II awareness.

- Underwriter
  - Needs: Risk policy updates, underwriting guidelines modules.
  - Use cases: New line addition, model change reviews, delegated authority refreshers.
  - Fit: Assigned to Underwriting BU with mandatory periodic recertification.

- Client Support Specialist
  - Needs: Systems training (ApexCRM, ServiceGate), escalation protocols.
  - Use cases: Case handling, SLA management, documentation standards.
  - Fit: Support org with KPI-linked training tracks.

Non-fit indicators:
- Contractors without NFG email or KeyShield SSO (use NFG-HR-TRN-EXT-002 contractor pathway).
- Regions under training freeze window due to regulatory audits (check Regional Variations).

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions

BANT/CHAMP prompts:
- Budget: Do you have a cost center and confirmed budget for external courses? What’s the maximum per learner?
- Authority: Who approves training requests? Manager only, or HRBP and Compliance too?
- Need: Which certification is gating your current pipeline (e.g., FI-Structured Credit L2)?
- Timing: What’s your deadline relative to client meetings or renewal dates?
- Challenges: Any past issues with course access, SSO, or content localization?
- Metrics: Which KPIs will improve (win rate, time-to-first-meeting, policy bind rate)?
- Red flags:
  - Request to waive mandatory compliance modules.
  - Mismatch between requested training and role/region (e.g., US annuities for UK-only RM).
  - Attempt to book external vendor training without internal equivalent justification.

# 4. Product/Policy Overview

Capabilities:
- LearnHub LMS delivers courses, tracks completion, issues digital badges.
- ApexCRM auto-updates certification badges via LearnHub webhook events.
- ServiceGate workflow for internal/external training requests with approval routing.
- DataBus streams training events for dashboards in InsightView.

Limitations:
- No offline completions; all must be tracked in LearnHub or uploaded proof to DocVault.
- External vendor CE credits require pre-approved mapping (HR-CAT-CE Map v3).
- Language support: EN default; DE/FR/ES available for core compliance only.

Dependencies:
- KeyShield SSO for access; PeopleBridge HRIS must have active job profile and region.
- DocVault retention enforced per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival (7 years).

Definitions:
- LMS: Learning Management System (LearnHub).
- CE: Continuing Education credits.
- Badge: Digital certification marker displayed in ApexCRM.
- RTO/RPO: Recovery Time Objective / Recovery Point Objective.

# 5. Pricing, Fees, and Discounting

Internal training (ENBL-TRN-001):
- Cost: $0 (internal chargeback applied to enablement budget).
- CE credits: Included where available.

External training (ENBL-TRN-EXT-002):
- Baseline fee per learner: $1,200.00.
- Volume tiers:
  - 10–24 learners: $1,050.00 per learner.
  - 25–49 learners: $950.00 per learner.
  - 50+ learners: $825.00 per learner.
- Add-ons:
  - Localization: +$250.00 per language.
  - Exam voucher: +$300.00 per voucher.

Discounting guardrails:
- Max discretionary discount: 10% by Manager.
- 11–20% requires Regional Director approval.
- >20% requires HRBP and Compliance sign-off with business case.

Approval Matrix (External Training and Exceptions):
- Level 1: Manager — Up to $5,000 total, ≤10% discount, SLA 2 business days.
- Level 2: Regional Director — Up to $25,000, ≤20% discount, SLA 3 business days.
- Level 3: HRBP + Compliance — Any amount, >20% discount, or regulatory-impacted content, SLA 5 business days.
- Level 4: CRO Office — Exceptions to mandatory internal-first rule, SLA 7 business days.

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow

This workflow governs training requests and certification gates for deal readiness.

Steps:
1) Submit Request
- System: ServiceGate > Form “TRN-Request-01”
- Required artifacts: Business need statement, target date, course link (if external), cost center.
- SLA: Ticket acknowledgment within 1 business day.
- Control: Requester must be listed in PeopleBridge with active role.

2) Eligibility Check
- Owner: Sales Enablement Ops
- Checks: Role-region fit, internal equivalent availability, CE mapping.
- Evidence: Eligibility checklist attached to ticket.
- Outcome: Approved to proceed or redirected to internal course.

3) Budget and Approval Routing
- System: ServiceGate auto-routes per Approval Matrix.
- Control: Discounts above threshold trigger HRBP/Compliance step.
- SLA: As per Approval Matrix.

4) Enrollment and Provisioning
- System: LearnHub course enrollment; KeyShield SSO verification.
- Evidence: Enrollment confirmation (ENR-ID).
- SLA: 3 business days from final approval.

5) Completion Capture and CRM Sync
- System: LearnHub webhook to ApexCRM; DocVault archival.
- Control: Completion event must include Course Code, Learner ID, Timestamp.
- SLA: CRM badge update <15 minutes; DocVault archival end-of-day batch.

6) Exception Handling
- Use Case: Missed deadline, course unavailability, LMS incident.
- Escalation: Tier 1 Enablement Ops > Tier 2 HRBP > Tier 3 Compliance > Tier 4 CRO.
- SLA: Workaround plan within 24 hours.

7) Close and Measure
- Artifact: Post-training survey; KPI baseline vs. post metrics.
- Evidence: InsightView dashboard updated within 2 business days.

# 8. Data and CRM Fields

Canonical fields (ApexCRM > User Profile > Certifications):

- learner_id (string, required): PeopleBridge Employee ID, e.g., PB-009876.
- course_code (string, required): e.g., TRN-COMP-100.
- course_name (string): e.g., Sales Ethics and Conduct.
- completion_status (enum): NOT_STARTED | IN_PROGRESS | COMPLETED | EXPIRED.
- completion_date (date): YYYY-MM-DD, e.g., 2025-11-30.
- expiry_date (date): YYYY-MM-DD, based on course policy.
- badge_id (string): e.g., BADGE-2025-13456.
- ce_credits (decimal(4,1)): e.g., 3.0.
- region_scope (enum): US | CA | UK | EU | SG | AU.
- verifier (string): Email of approver, e.g., priya.desai@nfg.com.
- docvault_ref (string): e.g., DV-TRN-2025-000112.
- source_system (enum): LEARNHUB | MANUAL_UPLOAD.
- last_synced_at (datetime): ISO8601, e.g., 2025-10-03T10:14:22Z.

Validation rules:
- completion_status = COMPLETED requires completion_date and badge_id.
- expiry_date must be >= completion_date.
- region_scope must include user’s PeopleBridge region.
- MANUAL_UPLOAD requires docvault_ref.

# 9. Integrations and APIs

LearnHub to ApexCRM Webhook:
- Endpoint: https://api.apexcrm.nfg.com/v1/training/completions
- Auth: HMAC with KeyShield service token (kid: KH-TRN-01).
- Event payload (example):
{
  "event_id": "EVT-TRN-2025-003421",
  "event_type": "COURSE_COMPLETED",
  "learner_id": "PB-009876",
  "course_code": "TRN-COMP-100",
  "badge_id": "BADGE-2025-55321",
  "completion_timestamp": "2025-10-02T16:11:00Z",
  "region_scope": "US",
  "ce_credits": 2.0,
  "docvault_ref": "DV-TRN-2025-000131",
  "signature": "b64(hmac)"
}

Response (success):
{
  "status": "ok",
  "crm_record_id": "CRM-CERT-100334"
}

Error patterns:
- 400 INVALID_REGION: region_scope mismatch with PeopleBridge.
- 409 DUPLICATE_EVENT: event_id already processed.
- 422 EXPIRED_COURSE: completion timestamp outside course validity window.

ServiceGate API for Request Status:
- GET https://servicegate.nfg.com/api/v2/tickets/{ticket_id}
- Returns SLA timers, approval stage, and assigned owner.

DataBus Events:
- Topic: nfg.training.completions.v1
- Consumers: InsightView dashboards, Compliance Monitor.

# 10. Procedures

A) Onboarding Training
1. Manager creates PeopleBridge profile and assigns job role and region.
2. HR triggers LearnHub auto-enrollment for mandatory modules (TRN-COMP-100, TRN-INFOSEC-101, TRN-AML-110).
3. Learner completes modules within 10 business days.
4. Control check: Compliance Ops reviews 5% sample weekly; evidence logged in DocVault (DV-TRN-AUDIT-YYYY).
5. Badge appears in ApexCRM; verify completion_status = COMPLETED.
6. Escalate overdue items (>10 business days) to Manager and HRBP.

B) Product Certification Prior to Deal
1. Identify required certification based on product in ApexCRM opportunity.
2. Search LearnHub for course code; enroll if not already assigned.
3. Complete course and exam; ensure badge_id issued.
4. Verify badge present on user profile in ApexCRM.
5. Control: No product pitch until badge visible. Evidence: Screenshot attached to opportunity under “Compliance Docs”.
6. If badge missing after 15 minutes, raise ServiceGate “TRN-Sync” incident; SLA 4 hours.

C) External Training Request
1. Submit ServiceGate TRN-Request-01 with:
   - Business case tied to opportunity ID or account plan.
   - Vendor link, syllabus, fees, dates.
   - CE mapping if applicable.
2. Enablement Ops checks for internal equivalent; if exists, reject external unless exception requested.
3. Approval routing per matrix; track in ServiceGate.
4. Upon approval, PO raised by Procurement; enroll learners.
5. After completion, upload certificate to DocVault; set docvault_ref on CRM certification record.
6. Control check: Finance reconciles PO vs. attendance; discrepancies escalated to HRBP.

D) Exceptions (Regulatory or Urgent)
1. If regulatory deadline imminent (<10 days) and course unavailable, open “TRN-Exception-02”.
2. Provide client impact and dollar risk estimate.
3. Compliance reviews and may authorize temporary supervised activity.
4. Evidence: Supervision plan uploaded to DocVault; expiry date enforced.
5. RTO: Plan issued within 24 hours; RPO: 0 hours (use current records only).

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix

- Objection: “I don’t have time for training before my client meeting.”
  Response: “We can fast-track the essential module (90 minutes) and your badge syncs to CRM within 15 minutes. It’s required under NFG-TRN-1001; I’ll schedule a slot today and notify your manager.”

- Objection: “External vendor is better than internal content.”
  Response: “We prioritize vetted internal modules mapped to our policies. If the external course adds unique CE value, submit TRN-Request-01; we can approve within 5 business days with a clear business case.”

- Objection: “The course isn’t in my language.”
  Response: “Core compliance is available in DE/FR/ES. For product tracks, we can add localization in 10 business days with approval; there’s a $250 localization add-on per language.”

- Objection: “I completed the course but no badge in CRM.”
  Response: “Sync is near-real-time. If it exceeds 15 minutes, I’ll log a TRN-Sync incident. We’ll meet the 4-hour RTO and can provide a DocVault proof for interim use.”

# 13. KPIs and Dashboards

Leading indicators:
- Time-to-Certification (median): Target ≤ 7 days from request to completion. Owner: Enablement Ops.
- Enrollment SLA adherence: Target ≥ 95% within 3 business days. Owner: HRBP Ops.
- Pre-meeting readiness (badge present before client meeting): Target ≥ 98%. Owner: Sales Managers.

Lagging indicators:
- Win rate uplift post-certification: +5% within 90 days. Owner: Sales Analytics.
- Policy bind rate for certified reps vs. non-certified: +8% differential. Owner: Underwriting Ops.
- Compliance exceptions issued: ≤ 2 per quarter. Owner: Compliance Ops.

Dashboards:
- InsightView > Training Readiness: by region, role, product.
- ApexCRM > Certification Coverage: account and opportunity overlays.
- Finance > Training Spend vs. Budget: variance ±5%.

# 14. Regional Variations

US:
- AML/CTF modules TRN-AML-110 mandatory annually.
- CE credits reported to FIN-ED registries via LearnHub integration.

EU/UK:
- MiFID II conduct training TRN-MIFID-200 required for advisors and RMs selling investment products.
- GDPR refresher TRN-GDPR-201 annually; data processing quiz included.

CA:
- Provincial licensing differences; track region_scope = CA and province in PeopleBridge.
- French language support prioritized for QC.

SG/AU:
- Cross-border selling rules TRN-CB-130 mandatory; localization available EN only at launch.

Audit windows:
- Training freezes 2 weeks pre-regulatory audit; exceptions require Compliance sign-off.

See also: NFG-COM-0009 (Compliance Training Catalog) for product-specific regional modules.
