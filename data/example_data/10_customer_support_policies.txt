# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)
- Who: All frontline teams engaging customers; Support Ops owns process; Deal Desk approves paid plan changes and credits.
- What: Standardized Support Plans (Standard, Premium, Enterprise), SLAs, escalation paths, communications, evidence requirements, and compliance statements.
- When: 24x5 baseline coverage; 24x7 for Severity 1 (Sev1) incidents and paid add-ons.
- Where: Log all cases in NFG-SM; link to account/opportunity in NFG-CRM; store artifacts in NFG-KB Space CX/Customers.
- SLAs (first response/resolve target):
  - Sev1 (critical outage): 15 min / 4 hrs (RTO 4 hrs; RPO 15 min)
  - Sev2 (degraded core function): 1 hr / 1 business day
  - Sev3 (minor impact): 4 business hrs / 3 business days
  - Sev4 (informational): 1 business day / 10 business days
- Do:
  - Classify severity using Definitions; confirm business impact and scope.
  - Use approved notification templates; update case every SLA interval.
  - Capture root cause, customer comms, and evidence.
- Don’t:
  - Commit SLAs beyond plan entitlements without Deal Desk approval.
  - Share personal data in chat/email beyond minimum necessary (NFG-PRIV-0022).
  - Close cases without customer acknowledgment for Sev1–Sev3.

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases
- Retail Investor Support (RI):
  - Use case: Portal access issues, statements, password resets.
  - Fit: Standard plan (SUPT-STD); phone and chat during business hours.
  - Non-fit: Custom runbooks or dedicated account managers.
- Institutional/Wealth (IW):
  - Use case: Portfolio data feeds, file transfer, reconciliation.
  - Fit: Premium plan (SUPT-PRM) or Enterprise (SUPT-ENT) with 24x7 add-on.
  - Non-fit: Uncontracted bespoke SLAs.
- Insurance/Underwriting Partners (UWP):
  - Use case: API down, policy issuance batch failures.
  - Fit: Enterprise plan + Dedicated AM (SUPT-ADD-DEDAM).
- Fintech Integrators (FTI):
  - Use case: API onboarding, webhooks, sandbox issues.
  - Fit: Premium plan with Developer-to-Developer channel.

Eligibility:
- Premium/Enterprise require annual contract ≥ $250,000 ARR or regulatory criticality classification “Tier 1/2” per NFG-BCP-0100.
- 24x7 add-on requires on-call contact rota from client and signed SSLA (Support Service Level Addendum).

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions
Use CHAMP-style prompts:
- Challenges: What is the business impact if this function is unavailable for 4 hours? Which processes are affected?
- Authority: Who signs the SSLA? Who owns incident comms on your side?
- Money: Do you have budget for 24x7 or dedicated AM? Are credits expected for past incidents?
- Priorities: Upcoming cutover dates? Regulatory reporting deadlines?

Red flags:
- Requests for RTO < 1 hr without HA/DR contract.
- No named technical contact; shared mailboxes only.
- Data residency hard requirement not matched by region (see Regional Variations).
- Expectation of proactive monitoring without API/alert integration enabled.

# 4. Product/Policy Overview
Capabilities:
- Multi-channel support: email (support@nfg.com), phone, chat, in-product help.
- Incident management with on-call rotations; automated paging via NFG-Notify.
- Status page: status.nfg.com with incident timelines and postmortems (redacted).
- Planned maintenance notifications ≥ 7 days notice (≥ 30 days for breaking changes).

Limitations:
- Configuration support only; no custom code development.
- Data restore: RPO commitments vary by plan; object-level recovery best-effort for Standard.
- No personal financial advice in support interactions.

Dependencies:
- Customer must maintain up-to-date contacts in NFG-CRM; enable API access for proactive alerts if contracted.
- For API issues, client must provide request IDs, timestamps (UTC), and correlation IDs (X-NFG-CID).

# 5. Pricing, Fees, and Discounting
Support Plans (annual, billed upfront):
- Standard (SUPT-STD): Included with platform; business hours, 24x5 for Sev1. $0 incremental.
- Premium (SUPT-PRM): 24x5, faster SLAs, Developer channel. $60,000/year.
- Enterprise (SUPT-ENT): 24x7 Sev1–Sev2, TAM quarterly reviews, runbook. $180,000/year.
Add-ons:
- 24x7 Coverage (SUPT-ADD-24x7) for Premium: $40,000/year.
- Dedicated AM (SUPT-ADD-DEDAM): $75,000/year.

Discounting Guardrails:
- Max standard discount: 10% Premium; 15% Enterprise; 0% add-ons.
- Multi-year (≥ 3-year) prepay: additional 5% with CFO approval.

Approval Matrix:
- Level 0: Within guardrails; Owner: Sales Manager; SLA to approve: 1 business day.
- Level 1: Exceed discount by ≤ 5% or credit ≤ $25,000; Owner: Deal Desk Lead; SLA: 2 business days.
- Level 2: Exceed discount by > 5% or credit $25,001–$100,000; Owner: VP CX + Finance; SLA: 3 business days.
- Level 3: Any SLA override, unlimited credits, or regulatory risk; Owner: CCO + CFO; SLA: 5 business days.

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow
Use for: Support plan changes, SSLA commitments, service credits > $5,000.

Steps:
1) Submit Request: Open NFG-SM case type “Deal Desk - Support” with fields: Opportunity ID, Customer Tier, Requested Plan, Discounts, Credits.
2) Artifacts Required:
   - Draft SSLA (from Section 12), customer use case, and incident history (last 12 months from NFG-DataLake).
   - Financial impact assessment (template DL-0300-T2).
3) Compliance Check: Deal Desk verifies alignment with NFG-PRIV-0022, NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.
4) Approval Routing: Auto-routed via matrix in Section 5 using approval rule DD-SUP-ALG-01.
5) Customer Communication: Use template CC-SUP-SSLA-01; no commitments until approvals recorded in NFG-CRM (field: Support_Plan_Status__c = Approved).
6) Contract Execution: DocuSign envelope ENVELOPE-2025-####; attach to CRM and NFG-SM.
7) Provisioning: CX Ops updates entitlement in NFG-SM and toggles plan feature flags via API (see Section 9).
8) Evidence and Closure: Upload approval artifacts; set Case Resolution Code = “Support Plan Change” and enter go-live date.

SLAs:
- Intake review within 4 business hours.
- End-to-end cycle target: 5 business days (standard), 10 business days (Level 2+).

Escalation Path:
- Day 2 pending: Escalate to Deal Desk Lead.
- Day 4 pending: Escalate to VP CX.
- Regulatory/board-visible clients: Notify CCO immediately.

# 7. Risk and Compliance Disclosures
Required statements:
- Data Handling: “For your protection, please do not send account numbers or PII in chat or email. Use the Secure Upload link in your portal.” (NFG-PRIV-0022)
- Recording: “Calls and chats may be recorded and archived per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.”
- Incident Comms: “Estimated times to resolution are targets, not guarantees, unless expressly agreed in your SSLA.”
- Advice Disclaimer: “Support provides technical assistance only and does not offer investment advice.”

Paperwork:
- Archive all comms and attachments via NFG-SM to WORM storage within 24 hours.
- Include post-incident RCA within 10 business days for Sev1–Sev2 (NFG-INC-0200).

# 8. Data and CRM Fields
Canonical Fields (NFG-CRM Account/Case):
- Account.Support_Plan__c (Picklist: Standard, Premium, Enterprise) — required; example: “Premium”
- Account.Support_RPO_Minutes__c (Number) — 15, 60, 1440
- Account.Support_RTO_Hours__c (Number) — 4, 24, 72
- Case.Severity__c (Picklist: Sev1, Sev2, Sev3, Sev4) — required
- Case.Business_Impact__c (Text 255) — required; example: “Trade confirmations delayed for 1,200 clients”
- Case.Environment__c (Picklist: Prod, UAT, Sandbox) — required
- Case.Correlation_ID__c (Text 64) — example: “CID-2025-10-03-000123”
- Case.Root_Cause_Code__c (Picklist: Defect, Config, 3rd-Party, User Error, Unknown) — required at closure
- Case.Resolution_Summary__c (Long Text 4000) — required at closure
- Case.Client_OnCall_Contact__c (Lookup Contact) — required for Sev1/Sev2
- Case.RCA_Required__c (Checkbox) — default true for Sev1/2
Validation:
- Sev1 requires Environment = Prod and Business_Impact__c length ≥ 50.
- If Support_Plan__c = Standard, disallow 24x7 add-on in Entitlement table.

Example Values:
- Account.Support_Plan__c = “Enterprise”
- Case.Severity__c = “Sev2”
- Case.Correlation_ID__c = “CID-2025-10-03-000457”

# 10. Procedures
A) New Customer Support Onboarding
1) Verify Contract: Confirm Support_Plan__c and SSLA in NFG-CRM.
   - Control: Compare contract fields to product_codes; evidence: screenshot in case.
2) Contacts: Collect primary/secondary contacts, on-call rota, and secure email.
   - Control: Validate business domain; evidence: Contacts added in CRM.
3) Channels: Enable client access to portal, chat; provide Secure Upload link.
   - Control: Test login; evidence: access confirmation ticket note.
4) Runbook: For Premium/Enterprise, schedule runbook creation workshop.
   - Control: Store runbook in NFG-KB; evidence: link in CRM custom field Runbook_URL__c.
5) Monitoring/Integrations: Offer Incident Webhook setup.
   - Control: Test 200 response; evidence: log snippet attached (see below).

Log Snippet (artifact):
2025-10-03T12:05:22Z INFO WebhookValidated cid=CID-2025-10-03-000501 acct=CUST-220019 status=200

B) Incident Handling (Sev1–Sev4)
1) Intake: Create NFG-SM case; set Severity__c; capture impact and timestamp UTC.
   - Control: If Sev1, auto-page on-call via NFG-Notify; evidence: page ID in case comments.
2) First Response: Acknowledge per SLA; include correlation ID.
   - Control: Timeboxed; evidence: Case timeline shows response within SLA.
3) Diagnose: Engage resolver groups (Apps, Infra, Data).
   - Control: Assign within 15 min for Sev1; evidence: assignment audit.
4) Communications: Send updates at SLA intervals (15/60/240/480 min depending on severity).
   - Control: Use CC-SUP-INC-01 template; evidence: comms attached.
5) Mitigation/Resolution: Implement workaround or fix; document.
   - Control: CAB approval for prod changes if outside emergency change policy.
6) Closure: Validate with customer; set Root_Cause_Code__c and Resolution_Summary__c.
   - Control: Manager review for Sev1/2 within 1 business day.

C) Data Restore Request
1) Validate Entitlement: Check RPO/RTO from Support_Plan__c.
2) Confirm Scope: Object(s) and timeframe; obtain CID.
3) Execute Restore: Coordinate with Data Ops via NFG-SM task.
4) Verify and Sign-off: Customer confirms; attach checksum.
- Controls: Access logged in NFG-DataLake; approvals per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.

D) Service Credit Issuance
1) Qualify: Confirm SLA breach via case audit.
2) Calculate: % of monthly support fee or platform fee per SSLA.
3) Approval: Route via Level 1–3 matrix.
4) Issue: Credit memo CM-YYYY-#### via Finance; attach to CRM.
- Control: Dual approval for credits > $25,000.

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix
- “We need 5-minute first response.” 
  - Response: Our fastest SLA is 15 minutes under Enterprise with 24x7. For 5 minutes, we require a bespoke on-call arrangement and engineering standby; this triggers Level 3 approval and higher fees. Let’s review your true business impact windows.
- “We expect free 24x7 coverage.”
  - Response: Standard includes 24x5 for Sev1. 24x7 requires staffing and on-call rotations priced via SUPT-ADD-24x7. We can explore a multi-year discount with VP CX approval.
- “Your RPO must be zero.”
  - Response: Zero data loss implies synchronous replication and premium storage. Our Enterprise plan offers 15-minute RPO. For RPO 0, we need a DR architecture review and separate HA/DR contract.
- “We need named engineers.”
  - Response: A Dedicated AM is available as an add-on. Named engineers fall under Professional Services Statements of Work; we can scope that separately.

# 13. KPIs and Dashboards
Leading indicators:
- % Cases triaged within 15 minutes (target ≥ 95%)
- % SLA updates on time (target ≥ 98%)
- Monitoring coverage (accounts with webhook enabled) (target ≥ 70% of Premium/Enterprise)

Lagging indicators:
- First Response SLA attainment by severity (target ≥ 99% Sev1, ≥ 97% Sev2)
- Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR) Sev1 (target ≤ 3.5 hrs)
- Customer Effort Score (CES) average ≥ 4.2/5
- Credits as % of support revenue (target ≤ 1.5%)

Ownership:
- CX Ops owns triage and updates; Deal Desk tracks credits; RMs own CES follow-ups.

Dashboards:
- NFG-CRM “Support Health” and NFG-SM “SLA Compliance” with filters by region, plan, severity.

# 14. Regional Variations
- EU/UK (GDPR, DORA): 
  - Data residency: tickets stored in EU region for EU accounts; cross-border transfers require SCCs (NFG-PRIV-0022).
  - Incident notifications to regulators may be required for material outages; coordinate with Compliance.
- Canada (OSFI): 
  - Critical third-party outages must be reported per TPRM guidelines; RMs must flag Tier 1 institutions.
- APAC (MAS): 
  - Maintenance windows must avoid local market hours; publish times in SGT.
- US (SEC/FINRA):
  - Record retention WORM storage minimum 7 years for broker-dealer communications (NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival).
