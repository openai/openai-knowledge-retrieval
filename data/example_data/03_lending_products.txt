# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)

- Who: For frontline advisors, RMs, underwriters, and support selling/servicing NFG Lending Products (codes TL-STD-001, LOC-REV-002, EQF-003, CRE-MORT-004, CON-PL-005).
- What: Standard loan types with configured pricing, covenants, and approval thresholds; offered via AuroraCore; documented in DocVault360; signed via SignBridge.
- When: Initial response to customer within 1 business day; term sheet within 3 business days post-complete app; credit decision SLA 5 business days standard (2 days expedited with fees).
- Where: Origination in Keystone CRM; underwriting in MeridianScore; funding via AuroraCore; servicing in LoanSphere.
- Do:
  - Run KYC/OFAC in SentinelKYC before quoting rates (NFG-KYC-0110).
  - Use pricing guardrails; obtain approvals per matrix.
  - Capture covenants and collateral in CRM fields.
  - Disclose APR ranges, fees, and risk-based pricing factors.
- Don’t:
  - Promise approval or rate without credit review.
  - Backdate documents or bypass e-sign evidence.
  - Split loans to avoid approval thresholds.
- SLAs: 
  - App completeness check: 4 business hours.
  - Credit decision: 5 business days.
  - Docs sent: 24 hours post-approval.
  - Funding: T+1 after countersignature.
- Escalation: RM -> Deal Desk Queue (DD-LOANS) -> Senior Credit Officer -> CCO.

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases

Segments:
- SMB Owner (revenues $2–$50M): Working capital (LOC-REV-002), equipment purchases (EQF-003).
- Mid-Market (revenues $50–$500M): Expansion CAPEX (TL-STD-001), acquisition financing (TL-STD-001 + LOC).
- CRE Investor/Developer: Stabilized or construction-to-perm (CRE-MORT-004).
- Consumer Prime: Debt consolidation and major purchases (CON-PL-005, unsecured).

Use Cases:
- Seasonal inventory build: Revolving LOC with borrowing base tied to A/R and inventory.
- Equipment purchase: Fixed-rate, asset-backed with 20% down and 60-month amortization.
- Real estate acquisition: 5/25 CRE mortgage with DSCR covenant.
- Personal loan via digital origination: 24–60 month terms with autopay discount.

Eligibility Fit:
- Time in business: ≥24 months (commercial), ≥12 months (consumer).
- FICO/Score: Consumer ≥680; Commercial PKI score (MeridianScore) ≥620.
- DSCR: CRE ≥1.25x; Term loan minimum global DSCR 1.20x.
- Leverage: Total debt/EBITDA ≤3.5x (non-CRE).
- Collateral: UCC-1 for equipment; first lien on CRE; personal guarantee for SMBs.

Non-Fit:
- Prohibited industries (NFG-RISK-0412): Unlicensed cannabis (US), payday lending, sanctioned entities.
- Startups pre-revenue (except venture program; see related_docs NFG-VENTURE-0105).
- Subprime consumer (score <620); high DTI >45% (consumer).

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions

CHAMP/BANT Prompts:
- Challenges: What problem does the capital solve? Inventory, payroll, acquisition?
- Authority: Who signs? Any board/bank covenants with other lenders?
- Money: Target amount, max monthly debt service tolerance, down payment?
- Priority: Timing drivers (close date)? Seasonality?
- Financials: Last 2 years financials and YTD? Cash flow trends?
- Collateral: Available assets? Any existing liens/encumbrances?
- Banking: Current bank relationships and maturities?
- Covenants: Comfortable with DSCR or leverage covenants?
- Exit: For CRE/construction, stabilization timeline and takeout plan?
- Consumer: Employment stability, housing, autopay setup?

Red Flags:
- Rapid recent leverage increases >1.0x.
- Tax liens or unresolved UCC filings.
- Unverifiable revenue; cash-only business without POS records.
- Mismatch between collateral value and ask; appraisal avoidance.
- Consumer debt consolidation but ongoing revolving usage.

# 4. Product/Policy Overview

Capabilities:
- TL-STD-001: Fixed/variable rate term loans; 12–84 months; amortizing; prepayment with 1–3% fee first 24 months.
- LOC-REV-002: Revolving, interest-only, annual renewal; borrowing base (A/R 80%, Inventory 50%).
- EQF-003: Equipment finance; 70–90% LTV; asset-tied; balloon option up to 20%.
- CRE-MORT-004: 5/25 or 7/25; LTV up to 70% (owner-occupied) / 65% (investment); DSCR ≥1.25x.
- CON-PL-005: Unsecured $5,000–$75,000; fixed APR; autopay 0.25% discount.

Limitations:
- Max single-obligor exposure: $25M (commercial); $100k (consumer).
- Prohibits negative amortization and interest-only on term loans >24 months (policy NFG-CREDIT-1202).
- No secondary lien positions on CRE except intercreditor-approved.

Dependencies:
- KYC/AML clearance (SentinelKYC).
- Collateral appraisal per NFG-APP-200 (CRE/EQF).
- Income verification via VerifiLink (consumer) or audited financials (commercial >$5M).

# 5. Pricing, Fees, and Discounting

Rate Guardrails (as-of 2025-10-01; subject to base rate AuroraPrime = 7.50%):

Product | Base Spread (over AuroraPrime) | All-in APR Range | Origination Fee | Prepayment | Floor Rate
-|-|-|-|-|-
TL-STD-001 | +2.00% to +5.00% | 9.75%–14.50% | 0.75%–1.50% | 3% yr 1, 2% yr 2, 0% thereafter | 8.75%
LOC-REV-002 | +1.50% to +4.00% (variable) | 9.00%–13.50% | 0.50%–1.00% + 0.25% renewal | None | 8.50%
EQF-003 | +1.75% to +4.50% | 9.25%–14.00% | 1.00% | 2% first 18 months | 8.75%
CRE-MORT-004 | +1.25% to +3.50% | 8.75%–12.00% | 0.75%–1.25% + third-party costs | 3-2-1 stepdown | 8.50%
CON-PL-005 | Risk-based fixed | 8.99%–18.99% | $95–$295 | None | 8.99%

Discounting Guardrails:
- Max discretionary rate concession: 50 bps by RM with Deal Desk approval; >50 bps requires Senior Credit Officer.
- Fee waivers capped at $500 without Product sign-off (CON-PL-005) or 25 bps of commitment (commercial).

Approval Matrix (Pricing/Structure):

Level | Authority | Conditions | Evidence Required
-|-|-|-
L1 RM | Rate concession ≤25 bps; fee waiver ≤$250 | No covenant weakening; LTV/DSCR within policy | Pricing Rationale Note in CRM; Email approval from Deal Desk
L2 Deal Desk Manager | Concession >25–50 bps; fee waiver ≤25 bps commitment | DSCR not <1.15x; LTV within -5% of policy | Deal Desk Case ID, MeridianScore output, Sensitivity excel
L3 Senior Credit Officer | Concession >50–100 bps; covenant adjustments; intercreditor | DSCR ≥1.10x; Stress DSCR ≥1.0x | Credit Memo, Legal Review Ticket, Appraisal
L4 CCO | Exceptions beyond policy; single-obligor >$15M | Board-level exposure; industry cap exceptions | CCO Memo; Policy Exception Form NFG-EXC-77; Committee minutes

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow

Step-by-step (Keystone CRM Case Type: DEAL-LOAN):

1) Intake (SLA 4h):
- RM creates Opportunity (OPP-YYYY-####) and Deal Desk Case (DD-LOANS-####).
- Attach financials, bank statements, and IDs to DocVault360 (min required checklist auto-generates).
- Control: SentinelKYC pre-screen must be “PASS” before pricing. Evidence: KYC Screen ID.

2) Preliminary Pricing (SLA same-day):
- Use Pricing Tool in Keystone (module PRC-LOAN) with guardrails.
- Control: System prevents sub-floor rates. Evidence: Pricing Snapshot export.

3) Underwriting Package (SLA 1 day):
- Submit to MeridianScore; retrieve PD/LGD and DSCR calculations.
- Control: Mandatory fields check; auto-stop if missing revenue or collateral values.
- Evidence: Underwriting Summary PDF.

4) Credit Decision (SLA 5 days):
- L1/L2/L3 approvals per matrix routed via Workflow WF-CR-APP.
- Control: Exception logic triggers NFG-EXC-77 form.
- Evidence: Approval audit log snippet stored in DocVault360.

5) Term Sheet (SLA 24h post-approval):
- Generate from template TS-LOAN-v5 in Keystone; send via SignBridge.
- Control: APR and fee disclosures auto-calculated; e-sign IP/Geo evidence stored.

6) Closing & Funding (SLA T+1):
- Title/UCC filings (for CRE/EQF) recorded in DocVault360 with filing receipt.
- Funding instruction in AuroraCore; dual-control release (Ops + RM).
- Control: Pre-funding checklist; covenant schedule confirmed.

7) Post-Close Boarding:
- LoanSphere boarding with product code and covenants.
- Control: First payment date confirmed; auto-draft (if opted) set.

Escalation Path:
- SLA risk or high-priority deals: Tag “Hot” in Keystone; notify Deal Desk Manager; if unresolved in 24h, escalate to Senior Credit Officer; 48h to CCO.

# 7. Risk and Compliance Disclosures

Required Verbal Disclosures (record notes in CRM):
- “Credit approval is subject to verification of information, collateral valuation, and final underwriting.”
- “Rates are risk-based and may vary based on credit profile, collateral, and market rates.”
- For consumer: “APR includes interest and certain fees; prepayment reduces interest but fees may be non-refundable.”

Written Disclosures:
- ECOA adverse action notices within 30 days if declined (NFG-ECOA-100).
- Fair Lending parity check logged (NFG-FAIRLEND-0300).
- Reg Z disclosures for CON-PL-005; TILA-APR calc auto-generated.
- AML/OFAC attestation for all commercial deals (NFG-KYC-0110).

Recordkeeping:
- All comms archived per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival, 7-year retention.
- RPO/RTO for DocVault360: RPO 15 min, RTO 4 hours (BCP-REC-200).

# 10. Procedures

Onboarding (Commercial):
1) Confirm KYC PASS in SentinelKYC. Control: Screenshot evidence attached.
2) Verify financials: 2 years audited + YTD; tie-out revenue and EBITDA. Control: Underwriter cross-check; attach tie-out worksheet.
3) Appraisal/valuation: Order via AppNet (CRE/EQF). Control: Independence attestation (NFG-APP-200).
4) File lien: UCC-1 via LienFlow; get receipt. Control: Dual review by Ops.
5) Generate term sheet TS-LOAN-v5; send via SignBridge. Control: E-sign audit trail.
6) Book loan via AuroraCore API; confirm LOAN-#### created. Control: Compare approved vs. booked terms.
7) Board to LoanSphere; verify payment schedule. Control: First payment letter sent.

Onboarding (Consumer CON-PL-005):
1) IDV and income verification via VerifiLink. Control: Match rate ≤10% variance vs stated income.
2) Fair Lending model parity check. Control: Run NFG-FAIRLEND-0300 report.
3) Provide TILA disclosures. Control: Auto-generated PDF timestamped.
4) Fund via ACH to verified account; RPO 15 min, RTO 4 hours if failure.

Servicing (All):
- Covenant monitoring quarterly; LoanSphere auto-ingests financials. Control: Breach event SLA: RM contacts client within 2 business days; remediation plan in CRM.

Exceptions Process:
1) Flag Exception_Flag__c true triggers NFG-EXC-77 form.
2) RM drafts rationale; attaches mitigants (additional collateral, guarantees).
3) Route to L3/L4 as required. Control: Exception log maintained, monthly review by Credit Policy.

Evidence Artifacts:
- Approval audit log snippet:
  [2025-09-30 14:12:05Z] APP-2025-1034 -> L2 Approved by D. Ortiz (Deal Desk) Rate Concession 35 bps; DSCR 1.32; Exception: None. DocVault Ref DV-88210.

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix

Objection | Response | Add’l Action
-|-|-
“Rate is higher than competitor.” | We price for speed and flexibility. With your DSCR at 1.28 and LTV 68%, the current offer reflects policy. I can request up to 50 bps review with Deal Desk if we secure quarterly reporting and autopay. | Open DD case; propose covenant enhancement for concession.
“I don’t want a personal guarantee.” | For SMBs, guarantees align incentives and reduce rate. We can explore limited guarantee or higher rate by ~50–100 bps. | Model both scenarios; obtain L2 approval.
“Appraisal takes too long.” | We can use expedited appraisal (3 business days) with a $1,250 rush fee. Funding within T+1 after clear. | Order AppNet rush; document client consent.
“Fees seem high.” | Origination covers underwriting and onboarding. If you consolidate deposits with NFG and set autopay, we can reduce by 25 bps. | Add deposit cross-sell requirement in term sheet.
“I need interest-only.” | Policy limits IO to 12 months on term loans. We can structure 12 months IO followed by 48 months amortization. | L2 approval; ensure DSCR stress remains ≥1.0x.

# 14. Regional Variations

US:
- Reg O insider lending limits apply (NFG-REGO-040). State filing times vary (UCC).
- Max consumer APR varies by state; system caps dynamically.

Canada:
- Interest Act disclosures; prepayment penalties must follow Canadian format.
- Quebec language requirements for documents.

UK/EU:
- Consumer: FCA CONC rules; cooling-off 14 days.
- GDPR: Data minimization; DSAR response within 30 days (NFG-GDPR-001).

CRE LTV/DSCR:
- US: LTV ≤70% owner-occ; DSCR ≥1.25x.
- UK: LTV ≤65%; DSCR ≥1.30x; interest coverage per PRA rules.
- CA: LTV ≤70%; DSCR ≥1.20x but stress test at +300 bps.
