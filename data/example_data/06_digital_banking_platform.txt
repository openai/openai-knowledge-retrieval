# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)

- What: NFG Digital Banking Platform (DBP) is our unified online/mobile portal and API suite for retail and SMB/commercial clients to manage deposits, payments, cards, and treasury services.
- Who: Retail consumers, sole proprietors, SMBs (1–250 FTE), lower-mid market (251–2,000 FTE) with basic to advanced treasury needs.
- Where: Web (bank.nfg.com), iOS/Android apps, and REST APIs (api.nfg.com/v1).
- SLAs:
  - Uptime SLO: 99.95% monthly; Incident P1 response 15 minutes; P2 1 hour.
  - Support: Frontline triage within 30 minutes (business hours), L2 within 4 hours.
  - Onboarding: Retail 1 business day; SMB 3 business days; Commercial 5 business days (post-KYC).
  - Payments cutoffs: ACH 18:00 ET; Wires domestic 17:00 ET; RTP 24/7/365.
- Resilience: RPO 5 minutes; RTO 30 minutes across dual-region data centers (us-east/us-west).
- Do:
  - Qualify on entitlements (dual approval, ACH limits, API usage) and data residency.
  - Use Approval Matrix for discounts >15% or ACH limits >$5M/day.
  - Capture use case tags in NFG-CRM “DBP Use Case” field.
- Don’t:
  - Promise custom SSO beyond SAML/OIDC standard.
  - Commit features not on DBP Roadmap without Product approval.
- Escalation: Deal Desk (deal-desk@nfg.com) for pricing/terms; L2 Support via Slack #dbp-l2; P1 incidents: on-call +1-877-NFG-HELP.

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases

- Retail Consumer (RET)
  - Needs: Account view, bill pay, P2P, card controls.
  - Fit: Low complexity, mobile-first.
  - Non-fit: International treasury, bulk payments.

- SMB Owner (SMB)
  - Needs: Multi-user access, ACH payroll, wires, positive pay, QuickBooks.
  - Fit: 1–250 FTE; monthly payments <$10M.
  - Non-fit: Complex liquidity sweeping, multi-currency pooling.

- Lower-Mid Market Treasurer (LMM)
  - Needs: Segregation of duties, entitlements, SSO, API integration for ERP.
  - Fit: 251–2,000 FTE; requires ACH same-day, RTP, SFTP reporting.
  - Non-fit: Global pooling across >10 jurisdictions; advanced trade finance.

Primary Use Cases:
- Payments: ACH credits/debits, wires, RTP, bill pay.
- Collections: Lockbox file ingestion, ACH debits, RTP requests for payment.
- Treasury: Positive pay, ACH filters/blocks, dual approvals, daily limit governance.
- Data/Reporting: Real-time balances, intraday transactions, statements via API/SFTP.
- Cards: Virtual card issuance (commercial), card controls.
- Developer: REST APIs for payments and reporting; webhooks.

Eligibility:
- Must pass KYC/AML (NFG-KYC-0110), sanctions screening (NFG-AML-023).
- Data Residency: EU clients provisioned in EU region (GDPR/PSD2).
- Risk Fit: ACH RDFI/NACHA return rates projected <1.0% (debits) and <10% (any).

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions

BANT/CHAMP prompts:
- Budget: What is your current monthly payments volume ($)? Any per-item fee targets?
- Authority: Who approves treasury entitlements and payment limits internally?
- Need: Which rails are required (ACH, wire, RTP)? Do you need dual approvals or SSO?
- Timing: Go-live deadline? Payroll/AR cycles? Cutoff dependencies?
- Challenges: Return rate issues? Fraud controls needed (positive pay, account validation)?
- Metrics: Success defined by straight-through processing %, time-to-post, API latency?
- Process: Current bank file formats? NACHA/ISO 20022? Batch sizes?

Red flags:
- Requires host-to-host mainframe protocols (no H2H; use SFTP only).
- Needs custom APIs beyond /payments and /balances.
- Demands per-transaction latency guarantees <100ms (DBP SLO p50 250ms, p95 800ms).
- ACH debit model with unverified consumer base (elevated fraud risk).

# 4. Product/Policy Overview

Capabilities:
- Web/mobile portal with role-based entitlements; SAML/OIDC SSO (NFG-Identity).
- Payments: ACH, wires (domestic/int’l), RTP, bill pay; same-day ACH.
- Controls: Dual approval, maker-checker, daily and per-transaction limits, device trust.
- Treasury add-ons: Positive pay, ACH filters/blocks, account reconciliation.
- Data: Real-time balances, intraday posted transactions, statement export (CSV/ISO 20022 camt.053).
- Developer: REST APIs, OAuth2 client credentials, webhooks (HTTPS, mTLS optional).
- Notifications: Email/SMS for approvals, exceptions, and payment status.

Limitations:
- No custom UI per client; theme is standard with logo upload.
- International wires: No exotic currencies outside G10 + select EM (see Regional Variations).
- No file-based host-to-host beyond SFTP+PGP.
- RTP max per transaction $1,000,000 (subject to change by network).

Dependencies:
- Requires active NFG deposit account(s) (DBP-CHK/DBP-SAV).
- Entitlement changes require admin and out-of-band MFA.
- API access requires signed DBP-API schedule and IP allowlist.

Discounting guardrails:
- Standard levers: Waive base fee for 3 months with 24-month term; volume-based rate tiers above $10M/mo ACH.
- Max frontline discretionary: 15% off platform and per-item fees.
- Bundled term discounts require 12+ month term and minimum ACH volume $5M/mo.

Approval Matrix:
- Level 0: ≤15% discount; ACH limit ≤$5M/day; RM approval; log in NFG-CRM (Approval_Notes__c).
- Level 1: 16–25% discount or ACH limit $5M–$20M/day; Deal Desk + Product Owner approval (ServiceNow catalog SRV-DBP-APP-001).
- Level 2: 26–40% discount or ACH limit $20M–$50M/day; VP Product + Regional Risk Manager; attach financials.
- Level 3: >40% discount or ACH limit >$50M/day; CRO + CFO sign-off; Legal review for custom terms.

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow

Step-by-step:
1) Qualify and capture in NFG-CRM:
   - Create Opportunity with Product_Code__c = DBP-BIZ/DBP-TREAS.
   - Complete DBP Qualification fields (see Data and CRM Fields).
   - SLA: Complete within 1 business day of discovery.

2) Pricing and Limits proposal:
   - Generate quote in CPQ (Q-DBP-####).
   - If discounts >15% or ACH >$5M/day, open ServiceNow SRV-DBP-APP-001.
   - SLA: Deal Desk turnaround 2 business days.

3) KYC/AML:
   - Submit application APP-YYYY-#### via Onboarding Portal.
   - Attach UBO docs; run sanctions (NFG-AML-023).
   - SLA: Retail 1 day; SMB 3 days; LMM 5 days post-docs.

4) Contracting:
   - Use DBP MSA + Schedule (DBP-API if applicable) via NFG-DocuSign template DS-TPL-DBP-01.
   - Control: Legal clause check for data residency and API rate-limits.
   - SLA: 2 business days for standard; 5 days with redlines.

5) Implementation:
   - Provision in NFG-Core and NFG-Identity; create Admin User.
   - Control: Dual control verification for entitlements via L2 Support.
   - SLA: 1–3 business days.

6) Go-Live:
   - Test payment $1.00 on each rail; validate approvals, limits, notifications.
   - Evidence: Attach test logs to Opportunity (File: GLV-DBP-<CustID>.pdf).
   - SLA: Same day after provisioning.

Escalation path:
- Pricing/terms: Deal Desk (Marcus Lee) → VP Product → CRO.
- Risk/KYC: Onboarding Ops → Regional Risk Manager → Head of Compliance.
- Technical: L2 DBP Support → SRE On-call → Incident Commander (P1).

- ACH_Daily_Limit_Requested__c (Currency): e.g., 20000000.00.
- Dual_Approval_Required__c (Checkbox): true/false.
- SSO_Required__c (Checkbox): true/false.
- Data_Residency__c (Picklist): US, CA, EU, UK.
- API_Access__c (Checkbox): true/false.
- API_Endpoints_Needed__c (Text): e.g., /payments,/balances,/webhooks.
- Accounting_System__c (Picklist): QuickBooks, NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Other.
- Return_Rate_History__c (Percent, 2dp): e.g., 0.85.
- Cutoff_Dependencies__c (Text): e.g., “Payroll Tue/Thu by 15:00 ET.”
- Risk_Rating__c (Picklist): Low, Medium, High (prelim by RM).
- KYC_Application_ID__c (Text): e.g., APP-2025-0345.
- Implementation_Complexity__c (Picklist): Standard, SSO, SFTP+Mapping, API+Webhooks.

Validation rules:
- If API_Access__c = true, then Data_Residency__c cannot be blank and API_Endpoints_Needed__c required.
- ACH_Daily_Limit_Requested__c > 5000000 requires Risk_Rating__c not High.
- SSO_Required__c = true requires Implementation_Complexity__c in {SSO, API+Webhooks}.

# 10. Procedures

Onboarding (Retail/SMB/LMM):
1) Create Opportunity and collect required CRM fields (Section 8).
2) Submit application in Onboarding Portal (APP-YYYY-####); attach IDs/UBO docs.
3) Sanctions screening auto-runs; if potential match, escalate to Compliance Queue (GRC-AML-Q-02).
4) Risk scoring in NFG-DataLake; ensure score ≤7 (scale 1–10) for standard path.
5) Provision DBP access in NFG-Identity; invite Admin User.
Control checks:
- Evidence: KYC_Application_ID__c populated; sanction clearance confirmation saved.
- Dual Control: Two separate operators must approve ACH limits in Admin Console.
- Audit: Capture provisioning log (LOG-PRV-<APPID>.txt) in Opportunity Files.

Servicing — Entitlement Change:
1) Client Admin requests via DBP portal or Support.
2) L1 validates requestor identity; L2 executes change in Admin Console.
3) Out-of-band verification call-back to authorized contact.
4) Document change in Case (CS-####) with Before/After entitlements.
SLA: 1 business day.

Exception — ACH Limit Increase (Urgent Payroll):
1) Client submits urgent request before 14:00 ET with payroll evidence (PDF).
2) RM opens ServiceNow SRV-DBP-EXC-ACH; selects reason “Payroll”.
3) Risk reviews return history and balance coverage; target ≤45 minutes.
4) If approved, SRE applies temporary limit until cutoff; revert next business day.
Control: Attach approval emails; record temporary change in NFG-Logs, Change ID CHG-DBP-2025-0144.

Incident Response (P1 outage):
- Detect via monitoring; auto-page SRE on-call.
- Status page update within 15 minutes; RM Advisory email via NFG-Notifications.
- RPO/RTO enforced per NFG-BCP-0901; failover if MTTR > 20 minutes.
- Post-incident report within 2 business days.

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix

- “Fees are higher than my current bank.”
  - Response: We can align fees to volume. With your projected $12M/month ACH, your per-item drops to $0.09, and we can waive the base fee for 3 months on a 24-month term, subject to approval.

- “We need custom UI and workflow.”
  - Response: We standardize UI to ensure security and supportability. You can configure roles, approvals, and limits, and extend via our APIs and webhooks for your internal workflows.

- “We require guaranteed sub-100ms API latency.”
  - Response: Our published SLO is p50 250ms and p95 800ms. For latency-sensitive flows, we recommend pre-scheduling batches and using webhooks to trigger downstream processes.

- “Our auditors require dual approval and positive pay.”
  - Response: Both are native. We’ll help you configure maker-checker, daily limits, and positive pay with exception decisions before 12:00 local time.

- “International wires to emerging markets?”
  - Response: We support G10 and select EM corridors. Let’s review your corridors; where we don’t have direct coverage, we can route via partner rails or suggest alternatives.

# 14. Regional Variations

US:
- Regulations: GLBA, NACHA; RTP via TCH; data residency US.
- Cutoffs as listed; same-day ACH windows per Fed schedule.

Canada (CA):
- Interac e-Transfer roadmap H2 2026; current: EFT batch with 21:00 ET cutoff.
- PIPEDA compliance; data stored in CA region upon request (DBP-CA).

UK:
- Faster Payments supported; cutoff N/A (24/7); CHAPS domestic cutoff 16:00 local.
- PSD2 SCA enforced; open banking APIs available via NFG-OB-UK base path.
- Data residency UK/EU; SSO with enforced step-up per SCA.

EU:
- SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) and SEPA Instant (SCT Inst) 24/7; fees vary; FX not applicable intra-SEPA.
- GDPR strict residency; consent records per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.

International Wires:
- Supported currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, CHF, SEK, NOK, DKK, SGD, HKD, MXN.
- Restricted corridors subject to sanctions lists; OFAC/UK HMT/EU consolidated.
