# Quick Reference (TL;DR)

- What: End-to-end Financial Advisory Process for onboarding, suitability, pricing, proposal, and ongoing reviews.
- Who: Advisors/RMs (frontline), Underwriting (risk/credit), Deal Desk (pricing/approvals), Compliance (suitability), Support (servicing).
- When: Initial discovery to signed agreement within 5 business days SLA (standard), 2 business days SLA (priority tier).
- Where: AtlasCRM (client/prospect), eKYC Portal (identity/AML), RiskCompass (risk scoring), DocVault360 (records), Quoter (pricing).
- SLAs:
  - Discovery to Proposal Draft: 2 business days.
  - Suitability Review: 1 business day (auto-approve if green), 3 business days (manual).
  - Pricing Approvals: within 1 business day for Level 1–2; 2 business days for Level 3+.
  - Account Opening: 1 business day post-docs.
- Do:
  - Capture BANT/CHAMP fields in AtlasCRM during first call.
  - Record calls via Nimbus when discussing products.
  - Use Quoter for all fees/discounts; never hand-calculate.
  - Document suitability rationale using NFG-SUIT-100 template.
- Don’t:
  - Don’t recommend products before KYC completion for regulated regions.
  - Don’t share personal contact info outside approved channels.
  - Don’t store client docs outside DocVault360.

# Customer Personas and Use Cases

- Mass Affluent (assets $250,000–$1,999,999)
  - Use cases: holistic planning, retirement income, tax-aware investing, advisory lite.
  - Fit: seeks ongoing guidance; moderate risk tolerance.
  - Non-fit: pure self-directed traders.
- High-Net-Worth (HNW) ($2,000,000–$25,000,000)
  - Use cases: discretionary portfolios, alternatives access, multi-generational planning.
  - Fit: complexity requiring bespoke portfolios.
  - Non-fit: unwilling to share financial statements.
- Emerging Professionals (assets $50,000–$249,999)
  - Use cases: goal-based planning, auto-invest, advisory lite.
  - Fit: growth orientation, digital-first onboarding.
  - Non-fit: ultra-short-term speculation requests.
- Business Owners (operating revenue $1,000,000–$50,000,000)
  - Use cases: cash management, retirement plans, key person insurance, liquidity events.
  - Fit: coordinated business-personal strategies.
  - Non-fit: crypto custody requests (unsupported).
- Trusts and Charities
  - Use cases: IPS-driven management, ESG mandates, governance reporting.
  - Fit: formal Investment Policy Statement (IPS) and board approval processes.
  - Non-fit: unincorporated associations lacking governance documents.

Eligibility non-fit examples: sanctioned persons/entities, PEPs without enhanced due diligence, unverified source of funds, requests for prohibited products (e.g., unregistered crypto tokens).

# Qualification and Discovery Questions

Use BANT+CHAMP prompts:

- Budget
  - What annual advisory fee range are you comfortable with?
  - Are there constraints on custodial platforms?
- Authority
  - Who must sign the advisory agreement? Any board/trustee approvals?
- Need
  - What primary goals: retirement, wealth transfer, income, philanthropy?
  - What events in the next 12–24 months could impact liquidity?
- Timing
  - Target date for implementing a portfolio or plan?
- Challenges
  - Prior advisory relationships? What worked/didn’t?
  - Tax considerations or concentrated stock positions?
- Metrics
  - How will you measure success (drawdown limits, income targets)?
- Red flags
  - Refuses risk questionnaire; insists on guaranteed returns; undisclosed crypto holdings; requests to backdate forms; asks to omit information on KYC.

Record all responses in AtlasCRM Opp/Client record using fields in Section 8.

# Product/Policy Overview

Capabilities:

- Advisory Models
  - Discretionary: NFG manages per IPS. Product: WM-ADV-DIS.
  - Non-Discretionary: Advisor recommends; client approves trades. Product: WM-ADV-MGD.
  - Planning Only: Financial plans without asset management. Product: WM-ADV-PLAN.
  - Advisory Lite: Digital guidance + human check-ins. Product: RB-ADV-LITE.
- Services
  - Risk profiling (RiskCompass), goal-based planning, tax-loss harvesting (where eligible), quarterly performance reviews, fiduciary reporting for charities/trusts.
- Limitations
  - No direct crypto custody; no private placements in Advisory Lite; alternatives limited to qualified purchasers; insurance planning requires INS-PLN add-on.
- Dependencies
  - eKYC clearance, Source of Funds verification, Suitability rating green/amber, signed Advisory Agreement stored in DocVault360.
- Policy Anchors
  - NFG-SUIT-100 (suitability), NFG-KYC-0110 (identity), NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival (records), NFG-DPP-120 (privacy), SEC Reg BI and FINRA 2111.

# Pricing, Fees, and Discounting

Standard advisory fee schedule (annualized; billed quarterly in arrears):

| Tiered AUM Band | Discretionary (WM-ADV-DIS) | Managed (WM-ADV-MGD) | Planning Only (WM-ADV-PLAN) | Advisory Lite (RB-ADV-LITE) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First $1,000,000 | 1.00% | 0.85% | $2,000 flat/year | $300 flat/year |
| Next $4,000,000 | 0.80% | 0.70% | n/a | n/a |
| Over $5,000,000 | 0.55% | 0.50% | n/a | n/a |

- Minimums: DIS $500,000; MGD $250,000; PLAN $1,000; LITE $300.
- Family aggregation available across related accounts under same household ID (HHID).
- Discount guardrails:
  - Max discretionary discount without approval: 10 bps (0.10%) for AUM > $2,000,000.
  - Planning fee discount up to 20% allowed for existing AUM clients.

Approval Matrix (discounts off standard):

| Level | Discount Range | Role Approver | Additional Conditions | SLA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | Up to 10 bps or ≤20% on PLAN/LITE | Regional Sales Manager | AUM ≥ $250,000 | 4 business hours |
| L2 | 11–25 bps or ≤35% on PLAN/LITE | Deal Desk Manager | Rationale + competitor quote attached | 1 business day |
| L3 | 26–40 bps or ≤50% on PLAN/LITE | Head of Wealth, Compliance concurrence | Margin analysis from Quoter | 2 business days |
| L4 | >40 bps or >50% on PLAN/LITE | Executive Pricing Council | Rare; requires CFO sign-off | 3 business days |

All discounts must be calculated and submitted via Quoter Quote ID (e.g., Q-2025-10384). Manual overrides prohibited.

# Deal Desk Workflow

Step-by-step with required artifacts and SLAs:

1) Discovery and Call Recording (Owner: Advisor)
- Capture in Nimbus (Call ID auto-linked to AtlasCRM via CTI).
- SLA: same-day logging.
- Artifact: Call transcript summary (Nimbus Note) tagged “DISCOVERY”.

2) KYC Initiation (Owner: Support)
- Launch eKYC from AtlasCRM (API: /kyc/start).
- SLA: within 1 business day of discovery.
- Artifact: KYC Case ID (KYC-2025-00XXX), identity docs uploaded to DocVault360.

3) Risk Profiling (Owner: Advisor)
- Administer RiskCompass questionnaire (RCQ-Form v3).
- SLA: 1 business day post-KYC.
- Artifact: Risk Score (0–100), category (Conservative/Moderate/Growth).

4) Suitability Assessment (Owner: Compliance)
- Auto-rule check using NFG-SUIT-100; amber cases require manual review.
- SLA: Green 4 hours; Amber 3 business days.
- Artifact: Suitability Memo (S-2025-XXXX) stored in DocVault360.

5) Pricing and Quote (Owner: Advisor, Deal Desk)
- Generate fees in Quoter; attach competitor quotes if seeking discount.
- SLA: 1 business day.
- Artifact: Quoter PDF, Quote ID.

6) Proposal and IPS Draft (Owner: Advisor)
- Prepare proposal with IPS draft (IPS-Template v2.4).
- SLA: 2 business days from risk profile.
- Artifact: Proposal PDF, IPS draft in DocVault360.

7) Approvals (Owner: Deal Desk/Compliance)
- Route via AtlasCRM Approval Flow (AF-2025-XXXXX).
- SLA: per Approval Matrix.
- Artifact: Approval log with timestamps; margin check.

8) Agreement and Account Opening (Owner: Support)
- Send Advisory Agreement (AA-Form v5) for e-sign.
- SLA: 1 business day post-approval.
- Artifact: Signed AA, Custody account numbers.

9) Implementation (Owner: Portfolio Ops)
- Fund transfers, model assignment, tax settings.
- SLA: 2 business days after funding.
- Artifact: Trade blotter ID (TB-2025-XXXXX).

10) First 90-Day Review (Owner: Advisor)
- Schedule check-in; verify onboarding satisfaction.
- SLA: within 90 days.
- Artifact: Review Note (REV-2025-XXXXX).

Escalation Path: Advisor -> Regional Sales Manager -> Deal Desk Manager -> Head of Wealth. Compliance escalations to Deputy CCO (Wealth).

# Risk and Compliance Disclosures

Required statements during conversations and in documents:

- Best Interest: “NFG acts in your best interest under applicable regulations, including Reg BI and MiFID II where applicable.”
- Fee Disclosure: Present fee schedule and any third-party costs; provide Quoter output.
- Conflicts: Disclose revenue-sharing or soft-dollar arrangements (see NFG-COI-022).
- Performance: No guarantees; present risks of loss; include drawdown history for models.
- Recording Consent: Advise calls may be recorded via Nimbus.
- Data Privacy: Reference NFG-DPP-120; obtain consent for data processing and cross-border transfer where relevant.
- Suitability: Document rationale per NFG-SUIT-100; retain evidence in DocVault360 (WORM).
- Archival: All communications and agreements archived per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival (RPO 15 minutes; RTO 4 hours).

# Procedures

Onboarding (standard)

1) Pre-Call Prep
- Review Prospect 360 in AtlasCRM; confirm sanctions screening pre-call (soft check).
- Control: Record screenshot evidence in CRM Attachments.

2) Conduct Discovery
- Use Qualification prompts; tag Nimbus call.
- Control: Complete Discovery Checklist in CRM; system enforces completion.

3) Initiate KYC
- Send eKYC link; verify doc receipt.
- Control: KYC Case auto-sync; Support verifies matches to legal name and DOB.

4) Risk Profiling and Goals
- Administer RCQ-Form v3; create at least 3 goals in CRM (retirement, education, cash reserve).
- Control: Risk_Score and Risk_Category must be present to proceed.

5) Suitability
- Submit Suitability Memo; Compliance auto check for product-code fit.
- Control: If Suitability_Status is Red, system blocks proposal.

6) Pricing and Proposal
- Generate Quoter quote; attach competitor analysis if discount >10 bps.
- Control: Approval flow triggers based on Discount_bps; prevents agreement until approved.

7) Agreement and Account Opening
- Send AA-Form v5 for e-sign through DocVault360 eSign.
- Control: WORM archive confirmation required; Account_Custodian cannot be set until AA archived.

8) Funding and Implementation
- Coordinate ACATS/transfer; assign model; confirm tax settings.
- Control: Two-person check on model assignment; Trade blotter requires IPS draft ID.

9) Post-Implementation Review
- Schedule 90-day review; log outcomes.
- Control: CRM task auto-created; Aging report sent to Advisor weekly.

Servicing

- Address change: Collect proof; update AtlasCRM and notify eKYC for re-verification if cross-border.
- Beneficiary change: Use Form BEN-2025; signature verification via eSign.
- Fee dispute: Open Case in AtlasCRM (CASE-2025-XXXXX); Deal Desk responds within 1 business day.

Exceptions

- High Concentration Positions (>20% single security)
  - Requires L3 approval and Client Acknowledgment Form CAF-2025.
- Illiquid Alternative Exposure (>15% of portfolio)
  - Requires Liquidity Risk Addendum and HNW qualification evidence.
- PEP/EDD Clients
  - Enhanced documentation; Compliance sign-off mandatory; annual review frequency increased.

Evidence Retention

- All steps must include artifacts in DocVault360. RPO 15 minutes, RTO 4 hours. Retention: minimum 7 years post-relationship.

# Objection Handling Matrix

| Objection | Response | Evidence/Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Fees seem high.” | Our tiered schedule reduces fees as assets grow. Here’s your quote: 0.95% blended, with potential 10 bps relationship discount. | Present Quoter Q-2025-10384; attach competitor quote if seeking L2 approval. |
| “I want guaranteed returns.” | We cannot guarantee returns. We can design a portfolio targeting lower volatility and capital preservation aligned to your risk profile. | Share model drawdown history; include RiskCompass rationale. |
| “I don’t want to complete KYC.” | Regulations require us to verify identity and source of funds before providing advice. The process is digital and usually takes under 24 hours. | eKYC link and status update; document refusal if any. |
| “Why so many documents?” | Regulations mandate clear disclosures to act in your best interest and to document suitability and fees. We streamline via eSign to reduce friction. | Provide AA-Form v5 and Suitability Memo summary. |
| “Another firm offered 30 bps less.” | We can evaluate a competitive adjustment within policy if you provide their written quote, ensuring we maintain service quality and compliance. | Attach competitor quote; route L2/L3 approval. |

# Regional Variations

NAMER (US/Canada)

- Reg BI and FINRA 2111 suitability apply. Signed Reg BI disclosure required.
- Tax-loss harvesting restricted in certain states due to state tax rules; refer NFG-TAX-STAT-009.

EMEA

- MiFID II appropriateness/suitability questionnaires mandatory; 24-hour cooling-off for remote agreements in some countries.
- Ex-UK clients: 10% depreciation reporting for portfolios with leverage triggers notification.

APAC

- Enhanced cross-border data consent; privacy notices include data localization clauses.
- Some jurisdictions require wet signature for trusts; use hybrid process with courier and DocVault scan-in.

Fee Caps and Minimums by Region

- EMEA fee cap: 2.00% all-in including platform fees.
- APAC minimum AUM for DIS: $750,000 equivalent in local currency.
