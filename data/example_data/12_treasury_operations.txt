# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)

- Who: Corporate clients with average collected balances ≥ $2,000,000; SMBs via packaged bundles require simplified onboarding.
- What: Treasury services (payments, liquidity, information reporting) delivered via Atlas TMS + NexusPay Hub; settlement via Orion GL.
- When: Standard onboarding 5–7 business days (KYC cleared). Fast-Track eligible ≤ $5M monthly volume in standard geos: 2–3 days.
- Where: Supported regions US, CA, UK, EU; RTP currently US-only (The Clearing House). SEPA Credit Transfer via EU branch.
- SLAs:
  - Deal Desk response: 4 business hours.
  - Pricing proposal: 1 business day (<$50M annualized volume), 2 business days (>$50M).
  - Implementation kickoff after signed term sheet: 1 business day.
  - Payment processing cutoffs: US wire 17:00 ET; ACH files 20:00 ET; SEPA 15:30 CET; RTP 24/7/365.
  - RPO/RTO (NFG-BCP-0007): RPO 5 minutes; RTO 30 minutes for payment initiation; 4 hours for reporting.
- Do:
  - Run sanctions/KYC checks before quoting custom pricing.
  - Align pricing tiers to margin guardrails (see Section 5).
  - Capture canonical CRM fields exactly (Section 8).
- Don’t:
  - Promise same-day high-value cross-border without FX booking.
  - Offer notional pooling (TRSY-NPOOL) to US-only entities (regulatory constraint).
  - Modify term sheets outside Deal Desk templates.

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases

- Mid-Market CFO/Controller (MME): $50M–$500M revenue, ACH-heavy AP/AR, needs balanced pricing and file integration. Fit for ACH, controlled disbursement, ZBA.
- Large Corporate Treasurer: $500M–$5B revenue, multi-currency, complex liquidity structures. Fit for cross-border, notional pooling (UK/EU), multi-entity overlays.
- Fintech/Marketplace Ops Lead: High transaction counts, API-first payouts/disbursements. Fit for NexusPay APIs, RTP, virtual accounts, fraud controls.
- Real Estate/Title: Escrow-heavy, high wire utilization. Fit for escrow sub-accounts, positive pay, wire templates.

Eligibility Fit:
- Minimum average balances: $2M (US), £1.5M (UK/EU).
- Monthly payment volume: ≥ 5,000 ACH or 300 wires, or ≥ $25M total throughput.
- Non-Fit/Deferral:
  - Sanctioned jurisdictions or adverse media flags (NFG-SANC-0301).
  - Cash-intensive businesses without enhanced due diligence.
  - Crypto-native custodians (handled by Specialized Financial Institutions team; See also DOC-TRSY-PRC-002).

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions

BANT/CHAMP Prompts:
- Budget: “What is your current monthly spend on treasury fees (wires, ACH, information reporting)?”
- Authority: “Who signs the treasury services agreement and the service fee schedule?”
- Need: “Which payment rails do you use today and where are settlement pain points?”
- Timeline: “Is there a regulatory or system-go-live date we should align with?”
- Challenges: “How do you manage liquidity across entities and currencies now?”
- Metrics: “What KPIs do you track (DPO/DSO, straight-through processing rate)?”

Red Flags:
- Requests for anonymous account structures or third-party payments without invoices.
- Exclusive need for US notional pooling.
- Commingling of client funds without escrow or trust framework.
- Refusal to enable sanctions screening or to provide UBO documentation.

# 4. Product/Policy Overview

Capabilities:
- Payments: Wires (domestic/international), ACH, RTP (US), SEPA CT (EU), BACS (UK).
- Liquidity: Zero Balance Accounts (ZBA), target balancing, end-of-day sweeps, EU/UK notional pooling (intercompany).
- Information Reporting: Prior-day, intraday via Atlas TMS and API webhooks.
- Fraud Controls: Positive pay, dual approval, IP allowlists, velocity thresholds.
- Escrow: Segregated accounts with sub-ledgering.

Limitations:
- RTP credit-only; max $1,000,000 per transaction; no cross-border RTP.
- Notional pooling available only in UK/EU under TRSY-NPOOL policy NFG-TRE-1021.
- Cross-border wires subject to FX booking via NFG Markets; cutoffs vary by corridor.

Dependencies:
- KYC/AML clearance (NFG-KYC-0110, NFG-AML-0202).
- Sanctions screening via Sentinel (NFG-SANC-0301).
- Record retention (NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival): 7 years for payment artifacts.

Definitions:
- Atlas TMS: NFG treasury portal and engine.
- NexusPay Hub: Payment orchestration and rail routing.
- Orion GL: Core ledger for settlement and fee assessment.
- RPO/RTO: Recovery Point/Time Objective under BCP.

# 5. Pricing, Fees, and Discounting

Pricing Table (USD unless noted):

- Domestic Wire: $12.00–$22.00 per item; cost floor $6.50; cutoff 17:00 ET.
- International Wire: $18.00–$38.00 per item + FX spread 8–20 bps; cost floor $10.00.
- ACH Credit/Debit: $0.07–$0.30 per item; cost floor $0.04.
- RTP: $0.75–$1.50 per item; cost floor $0.50.
- ZBA/Target Sweeps: $150.00 monthly per structure.
- Notional Pooling (EU/UK): 10–25 bps on net credit; legal documentation fee £2,500 setup.
- Information Reporting/API: $250.00 monthly + $0.002 per webhook event after 100k.

Discounting Guardrails:
- Minimum margin: 35% above cost floor without VP approval.
- Bundled discount up to 15% for clients with ≥ $100M average balances.
- Strategic logo exception requires CRO approval and clawback clause.

Approval Matrix:

- Level 1 (RM/Advisor): Discount ≤ 10% from list; ensure margin ≥ 40%; evidence: Pricing Calculator PDF, CRM note.
- Level 2 (Deal Desk Lead): Discount > 10% to ≤ 20%; margin ≥ 35%; requires Competitive Justification Memo; SLA 1 business day.
- Level 3 (Head of Treasury Ops): Discount > 20% to ≤ 30%; must show payback < 9 months; SLA 2 business days.
- Level 4 (CRO + Compliance): Any discount > 30% or policy exceptions (e.g., bespoke pooling); SLA 3 business days; requires Risk Impact Assessment (RIA-TRSY-##).

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow

Steps:
1) Intake (SLA 4h): Submit DEAL-REQ in Salesforce (Record Type: Treasury), attach recent statements, volume file, and signed NDA.
2) Sanctions Pre-Check (SLA 4h): Sentinel quick screen on legal entities and key officers; evidence stored in Helios Case ID HEL-TRSY-####.
3) Pricing Build (SLA 1–2 days): Deal Desk uses Pricing Engine v3.4; attach output PDF to Salesforce and tag Product Codes.
4) Legal Review (as needed; SLA 2 days): Standard terms via Treasury Services Agreement v2025.1; deviations logged in Contract Companion.
5) Client Presentation: RM delivers pricing and implementation plan; capture verbal acceptance notes in CRM.
6) Approval: Route via Approval Matrix; capture approvals in Salesforce Approval History; non-compliant deals auto-rejected.
7) Term Sheet Issuance: Generate via Term Sheet Template (Section 12); DocuSign envelope ID linked.
8) Implementation Kickoff (SLA 1 day post-signature): PM assigned, project plan in Atlas TMS; IT tickets created in JIRA TSY-####.
9) Go-Live Readiness Check: Control checklist signed by Ops Supervisor; evidence stored in Argus Data Lake bucket argus://trsy/go-live/CLIENTID.
10) Post-Go-Live Review (Day 30): Validate KPIs and fee realization; variance report to RM and Ops.

Escalation Path:
- Deal Desk Lead → Head of Treasury Ops → CRO; Compliance consult at any level for sanctions/KYC or RTS constraints.

# 7. Risk and Compliance Disclosures

Required Talking Points:
- “All payments are subject to sanctions and AML screening. NFG may delay or reject transactions to comply with laws.”
- “For cross-border, FX rates are indicative until booked and may change.”
- “RTP payments are irrevocable; ensure controls and recipient validation.”

Paperwork Disclosures:
- Sanctions & AML Acknowledgement (Form TRSY-SAN-01).
- Recordkeeping Consent under NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.
- Service Interruption Clause referencing NFG-BCP-0007 (RPO/RTO commitments).

Controls:
- Dual approval mandatory for wires > $100,000 unless explicit waiver by Risk (RIA-TRSY).
- Positive pay required for check disbursement clients.
- Beneficiary account validation (via Payee Confirm) for first-time wires.

- 429 Too Many Requests: Throttle to 100 TPS burst, 20 TPS sustained.

# 10. Procedures

Onboarding (Standard)
1) Pre-Screen (RM): Capture CRM fields; run Sentinel quick check; set Sanctions_Precheck_Status__c to “Clear”.
2) KYC Package (Underwriting): Collect KYB/KYC docs (articles, UBO IDs per NFG-KYC-0110); log in Helios.
3) Risk Rating (Compliance): Compute inherent risk; if Medium/High, require Enhanced Due Diligence checklist.
4) Contracting (Legal): Issue TSA v2025.1; add Service Fee Schedule; ensure sanctions clause present.
5) Technical Setup (Ops/IT): Provision Atlas TMS; enable products; API keys via Atlas Developer Console.
6) Controls Verification (Ops Supervisor): Validate dual approvals, IP allowlist, velocity limits.
7) Go-Live Call (PM + Client): Confirm cutoffs and file formats; record in project journal.

Control Checks & Evidence:
- Evidence of UBO verification stored in Helios under KYC-CASE ##.
- Atlas TMS audit log snippet attached to Argus go-live folder.
- Dual-approval test payment $0.01 executed and cancelled; log retained.

Servicing
1) Payment Exception: Client flags issue via Atlas or RM; create Helios ticket; attach transfer_id.
2) Investigation (Ops): Review NexusPay trace; check Sentinel sanctions logs.
3) Resolution: If bank reject, re-send or credit back; communicate root cause; update client within 4 business hours.
4) Post-Mortem (> $1M impact or > 2h delay): Open Incident INC-TRSY-####; follow NFG-INC-0302.

Exceptions
- Policy Waiver: Submit RIA-TRSY form for any deviation (e.g., single approver on RTP); Head of Ops must sign.
- Rapid Onboarding (< 48h): Allowed for existing clients with ACB ≥ $10M; Compliance approval required.

BCP/DR
- Failover to secondary data center on Atlas/NexusPay within RTO 30 minutes; client IPs unchanged (global anycast).
- RPO 5 minutes via synchronous replication; reconcile ledger deltas post-recovery.

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix

- “Your wire fees are higher than Bank X.”
  Response: “We can align tiers based on your exact volumes and our fraud controls. With your 300 wires/month, we can target $14.00/item subject to 35% margin. I’ll route this through Deal Desk today.”

- “RTP is too risky since it’s irrevocable.”
  Response: “Agreed RTP requires strong controls. We enforce dual approval and velocity limits by default, and offer payee validation. We can cap per-transaction at $250k initially and scale with you.”

- “We need US notional pooling.”
  Response: “US regulations limit notional pooling. We can implement ZBA and intercompany sweeps domestically, and offer notional pooling from our UK/EU entities if your structure permits.”

- “Our finance team can’t wait 2 weeks to integrate.”
  Response: “We support file-based onboarding in 2–3 days with CSV/ISO 20022, then transition to APIs. Implementation kickoff is next business day after signature.”

# 13. KPIs and Dashboards

Leading Indicators:
- Onboarding Cycle Time (target ≤ 7 business days; owner: Ops PM).
- Sanctions False Positive Rate (target ≤ 1.5%; owner: Compliance).
- API STP Rate (target ≥ 98%; owner: NexusPay Product).

Lagging Indicators:
- Fee Realization vs Proposal (target ≥ 95%; owner: Deal Desk).
- Payment Availability (target ≥ 99.95%; owner: SRE).
- Incident MTTR (target ≤ 45 minutes; owner: Ops).

Dashboards (Atlas Insights):
- Treasury Sales Funnel: conversion by segment and product code.
- Payment Rail Utilization: ACH/Wire/RTP mix by client; alerts on abnormal shifts.
- Liquidity Balances: average collected balances vs commitments.

# 14. Regional Variations

US:
- RTP via The Clearing House; $1,000,000 cap; FedNow pilot scheduled Q1 2026.
- Same-Day ACH cutoff windows: 10:30, 14:45, 16:45 ET; premium pricing applies.

UK/EU:
- SEPA CT and BACS supported; IBAN structure validation mandatory.
- Notional pooling permitted with intercompany agreements; withholding tax implications reviewed by Tax (NFG-TAX-2204).

Canada:
- EFT supported; wires via LVTS; RTP equivalent under review (Payments Canada RTR).
- Data residency: client artifacts stored in CA region per NFG-DATA-1201.

APAC (Limited Pilot):
- Cross-border only; no domestic RTP; additional due diligence for CN/HK corridors.
