# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)
- What: Investment Account products (Brokerage, Advisory, Margin, Tax-Advantaged) offered via Helix; surfaced in NFG Orion; serviced by Wealth Ops.
- Who: Individuals, joint, trusts, corporate, and retirement plans (regional eligibility applies).
- When: New accounts in 1–2 business days (SLA); funding in T+0 for wires, T+2 for ACH; trading immediately upon funding clearance.
- Where: US/CA/UK/EU/APAC; product variants differ per region (see Section 14).
- SLAs:
  - Account opening review: 8 business hours (Ops L2).
  - Suitability review (Advisory): 4 business hours (Compliance L2).
  - Transfer of Assets (ACAT/ATON): 7–10 business days.
  - Statement/confirm generation: T+1 by 08:00 local.
- Do:
  - Use Atlas CRM Product record with product_code mappings.
  - Collect KYC (NFG-KYC-0110) and Suitability (NFG-SUIT-0205) before presenting recommendations.
  - Route discounts via Deal Desk if exceeding guardrails (Section 5).
- Don’t:
  - Promise execution priority or guaranteed returns.
  - Place trades in advisory accounts without IPS/model alignment.
  - Waive regulatory or custody fees.

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases
- Mass Affluent Individuals ($250k–$1.5M)
  - Use: Core Brokerage (BRK-STD); auto-invest; fractional shares.
  - Fit: DIY with guidance; low fee sensitivity; wants digital + advisor.
  - Non-fit: Complex estates, concentrated single-stock risk.

- High-Net-Worth (HNW) ($1.5M–$20M)
  - Use: Advisory Discretionary (ADV-MGD) with model portfolios and tax-loss harvesting.
  - Fit: Delegates to PM team; holistic planning.
  - Non-fit: Day trading or options-heavy strategies in advisory.

- Small Business/Corporate Treasury
  - Use: Corporate Brokerage with cash sweeps; short-duration bond ladders.
  - Fit: Surplus cash management; policy-constrained investments.
  - Non-fit: Illiquid alternatives requiring capital calls.

- Retirement Savers
  - Use: IRA-TAX (US), RRSP (CA), SIPP (UK), ISA/JISA (UK), with low-cost ETF models.
  - Fit: Tax-advantaged, automatic contributions.
  - Non-fit: Margin or uncovered options.

- Active Traders
  - Use: Margin Enhanced (MRGN-ENH), real-time data, options enabled upon approval.
  - Fit: Experience verified; risk tolerance “Aggressive.”
  - Non-fit: New-to-investing or low liquidity buffers.

Eligibility Fit Indicators:
- Verified identity and source of funds (Atlas KYC status = Verified).
- Risk tolerance matches product type.
- Minimums met (Section 4).

Non-Fit Red Flags:
- Inconsistent stated objectives vs. requested features (e.g., capital preservation + margin).
- Unwillingness to provide tax residency or beneficial ownership (NFG-KYC-0110).
- Negative suitability outcome (Sentinel score < 65).

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions
Use CHAMP-style prompts:
- Challenges: What investment challenges are you solving in the next 12 months?
- Authority: Who signs the custodial/account agreements? Any investment committee?
- Money: What investable assets today? Planned contributions/withdrawals?
- Priorities: Liquidity needs, drawdown tolerance, tracking error tolerance?

BANT details:
- Budget: Target advisory fee cap? Sensitivity to transaction commissions?
- Authority: Decision-maker(s) and timeframe?
- Need: Is discretion desired? Tax-loss harvesting important?
- Timeline: Funding event (bonus, vesting, liquidity) date?

Red Flags:
- Requests for performance guarantees.
- Refusal to accept digital statements (violates NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival consent model).
- Margin request without income verification (NFG-MRGN-1002).

# 4. Product/Policy Overview
Products:
- BRK-STD (Core Brokerage)
  - Capabilities: Equities/ETFs/fixed income/mutual funds; fractional trading; DRIP; cash sweep.
  - Limitations: No discretionary trading; options level 1–3 with approval.
  - Minimum: $0.

- ADV-MGD (Advisory Discretionary)
  - Capabilities: Model portfolios, IPS, rebalancing, tax-loss harvesting, ESG screens.
  - Limitations: No individual security speculation outside IPS; performance fees prohibited in US retail.
  - Minimum: $100,000.

- MRGN-ENH (Margin)
  - Capabilities: Borrowing against eligible securities, intraday buying power; portfolio margin for qualified clients.
  - Limitations: No margin in tax-advantaged accounts; concentration and house maintenance requirements apply.
  - Minimum: $2,000 (US Reg T); higher for portfolio margin ($100,000+).

- IRA-TAX / SIPP/ISA/RRSP
  - Capabilities: Tax-advantaged wrappers; contribution automation; beneficiary designations.
  - Limitations: No shorting/margin; contribution limits per region.

Dependencies:
- Systems: Helix trade/settlement; LedgerX fee computation; Vault statements.
- Controls: Sentinel suitability/option approval; KYC via Atlas; e-sign via DocuBridge.

Definitions:
- IPS: Investment Policy Statement defining objectives, risk, constraints.
- DRIP: Dividend Reinvestment Plan.
- RPO/RTO: Recovery Point/Time Objectives for service continuity.

Service Continuity:
- RPO: 5 minutes (Helix order/trade data via Graphite CDC).
- RTO: 1 hour for NFG Orion; 4 hours for LedgerX (billing).

# 5. Pricing, Fees, and Discounting
Standard Fees (USD unless noted):

| Component | BRK-STD | ADV-MGD | MRGN-ENH | IRA-TAX/SIPP/ISA/RRSP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advisory Fee (AUM) | N/A | 0.85% ≤ $1M; 0.70% $1–5M; 0.50% >$5M | N/A | N/A |
| Trading Commission | $0 equity/ETF; $19.95 mutual fund; $0 bonds via spread | Included | $0 equity/ETF; $19.95 MF | $0 equity/ETF; $19.95 MF |
| Options | $0.65/contract | $0.50/contract | $0.50/contract | Not permitted in most tax wrappers |
| Margin Interest | N/A | N/A | SOFR + 5.00% (≤$100k), + 3.50% ($100k–$1M), + 2.25% (>$1M) | N/A |
| Account Maintenance | $0 | $0 | $0 | $25/yr (waived >$10k balance) |
| Transfer (ACAT/ATON) | $75 out | $75 out | $75 out | $75 out |
| Paper Statement | $3/mo | $3/mo | $3/mo | $3/mo |

Discounting Guardrails:
- Advisory Fee Floor: 0.40% blended floor without approval.
- Options Per-Contract Floor: $0.35 without approval.
- Margin Rate Floor: SOFR + 1.75% absolute floor with CFO exception only.

Approval Matrix (Discounts/Concessions):
- Level 0 (No approval): Within guardrails above; document rationale in Atlas “Pricing Notes.”
- Level 1: Up to 20 bps advisory discount below list, or options fee to $0.30/contract. Approver: Regional Sales Manager (RSM). SLA: 8 business hours.
- Level 2: 21–35 bps advisory discount, or bundled fee waivers (e.g., ACAT out fee waived). Approver: Deal Desk Director + Compliance L2. SLA: 2 business days.
- Level 3: >35 bps advisory discount, margin rate below SOFR + 2.00%, or multi-year pricing commitments. Approver: VP Wealth Product + CFO delegate. SLA: 5 business days.

Document all concessions in LedgerX “Pricing Override” record with override_id.

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow
Step-by-step:
1) Pre-Qualification (Advisor; SLA same-day)
- Complete Atlas KYC and Suitability questionnaires; ensure Atlas_KYC_Status = Verified.
- Run Sentinel suitability; target score ≥ 70.
- Artifact: Atlas Opportunity (OPP-YYYY-####), attach IPS draft if Advisory.

2) Pricing Request (Advisor to Deal Desk; SLA 1 business day acknowledgment)
- Submit Pricing Ticket in Orion: /orion/dealdesk/new with product_code and requested discounts.
- Required fields: client_segment, AUM_estimate, concessions_requested, competitive_context.
- Artifact: Pricing Ticket ID PT-2025-####.

3) Review and Approval (Deal Desk + Compliance; SLAs per Approval Matrix)
- Deal Desk validates revenue impact in LedgerX sandbox.
- Compliance checks NFG-SUIT-0205 and NFG-ADV-2040 (marketing entanglement rules).
- Outcome: Approval/Reject with conditions recorded in PT.

4) Term Sheet Issuance (Advisor; SLA 1 business day)
- Generate Term Sheet from Orion Template (Section 12).
- E-sign via DocuBridge; store in Vault with retention per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.

5) Account Opening (Wealth Ops; SLA 8 business hours)
- Submit application via Helix; ensure KYC artifacts attached.
- Funding instructions provided (ACH/Wire). For ACAT, initiate via Helix Transfers.

6) Post-Activation Audit (Compliance L1; SLA T+3)
- Verify pricing applied in LedgerX vs. Term Sheet.
- Sentinel monitoring activation; options approval if applicable.

Escalation Path:
- Deal Desk SLA breach: Escalate to Daniel Ortiz; if >24h over SLA, escalate to VP Wealth Product.
- Compliance block: Escalate to CCO delegate (M. Kowalska).

- “Investments involve risk, including loss of principal. Past performance is not indicative of future results.” (NFG-DISC-1001)
- “Margin borrowing increases risk and may result in losses greater than your initial investment.” (NFG-MRGN-1002)
- “Advisory services are provided on a discretionary basis as outlined in your IPS and Form ADV.” (NFG-ADV-2040)
- “Tax-advantaged accounts have contribution limits and penalties for early withdrawals; consult a tax advisor.” (NFG-TAX-3003)
- Recordkeeping: All marketing materials must be archived in Vault per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival. No off-channel communications (NFG-COMMS-0909).

Paperwork and acknowledgments:
- Form CRS (US), KID (EU), PRIIPs where applicable.
- Margin Agreement and Risk Disclosure Statement (MRGN-ENH).
- Options Agreement (levels 1–3) with experience attestation.

# 10. Procedures
Onboarding — Advisory (ADV-MGD):
1) Discovery and IPS Draft
- Control: Ensure suitability_score ≥ 70; evidence: Sentinel report attached in Atlas.
2) Pricing Approval (if needed)
- Control: Pricing tier L1+ approval artifact PT-####; evidence: PT attachment in Atlas.
3) Application Submission
- Control: KYC Verified; evidence: Atlas_KYC_Status = Verified; DocuBridge e-sign complete.
4) Funding and Activation
- Control: Funding verified in Helix; evidence: ACH/Wire confirmation or ACAT status “Completed.”
5) Post-Activation Review
- Control: Fee schedule applied matches Term Sheet; evidence: LedgerX webhook in Vault.

Servicing — Margin Increase (MRGN-ENH):
1) Client Request Intake
- Control: Confirm options_level and margin_eligibility; evidence: Atlas Account Profile.
2) Income/Net Worth Verification
- Control: Collect updated financials; evidence: DocuBridge upload; follow NFG-MRGN-1002.
3) Risk Reassessment
- Control: Sentinel re-score; threshold ≥ 75 for portfolio margin.
4) Approval and Update
- Control: Underwriter approval for house limits; evidence: Underwriting note in Helix.
5) Confirm Disclosures
- Control: Margin risk disclosure re-acknowledged; evidence: disclosure_ack_ids updated.

Exceptions — Advisory Fee Below Floor:
1) Advisor submits PT with rationale and competitor proof.
2) Deal Desk quantifies revenue impact (LedgerX sandbox model).
3) Compliance reviews potential conflicts (NFG-ADV-2040).
4) VP Wealth Product approval if >35 bps discount.
5) Apply pricing_override_id in Helix and LedgerX; confirm webhook in Vault.

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix
- “Why should I pay an advisory fee when trading is free?”
  - Response: Advisory includes ongoing portfolio management, rebalancing, tax-loss harvesting, and IPS-driven oversight. Trading is a tool; advice and management deliver outcomes. We align fees with assets and tier down as you grow. Form ADV details services and costs.

- “Can you guarantee performance if I choose the aggressive model?”
  - Response: We cannot guarantee performance. Your IPS and model target risk/return over a cycle; we monitor and rebalance. Required: Read NFG-DISC-1001.

- “I want margin in my IRA.”
  - Response: Regulations prohibit margin in tax-advantaged accounts. Alternatives include limited-purpose credit lines outside the IRA. We can discuss liquidity strategies compliant with NFG-TAX-3003.

- “Competitor offers 0.30% advisory fee.”
  - Response: We can evaluate pricing within guardrails based on scope and AUM. Provide the written offer; we’ll submit to Deal Desk. Minimum blended floor is 0.40% without Level 2 approval.

- “I don’t want to provide source-of-funds info.”
  - Response: We’re required by KYC rules (NFG-KYC-0110) to verify identity and source of funds to protect clients and the firm. We cannot open accounts without it.

# 14. Regional Variations
US:
- Reg BI applies; Form CRS mandatory.
- IRA contribution limits per IRS; no margin in IRAs.
- ACAT transfers supported.

Canada:
- IIROC suitability rules; RRSP/TFSA available.
- ATON transfers; Canadian-dollar pricing; HST/GST may apply to fees.

UK:
- FCA COBS; SIPP/ISA/JISA wrappers; stamp duty on certain UK equity trades.
- PRIIPs KID required for packaged products.

EU:
- MiFID II; appropriateness testing for complex products.
- PRIIPs KID and ex-ante cost disclosures; translations required.

APAC (SG):
- MAS FAA; suitability and risk profiling strict; SRS (Supplementary Retirement Scheme) supported.
- No options in certain markets; check local lists (NFG-MKT-ALLOW-APAC).

Operational:
- Settlement cycles differ (e.g., T+2 US/EU; verify per market).
- FX conversions: 10 bps spread applied; disclosure per region.
