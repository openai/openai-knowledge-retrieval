# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)

- Who we are: Northbridge Financial Group (NFG), founded 1987 in Boston, MA; diversified financial services across Wealth & Investment (NFG-INV), Commercial Lending (NFG-LEND), Fiduciary & Trust (NFG-TRUST), and Payments & Treasury (NFG-PAY); 18,400 employees in 26 countries.
- What we stand for: “Advise with clarity. Execute with rigor.” Culture anchored in compliance-by-design and client outcomes.
- Where we operate: US, Canada, UK, EU, Singapore, Australia; booking entities include NFG Bank N.A. (US OCC), NFG Securities LLC (FINRA/SEC), NFG Payments Ltd (FCA).
- Core SLAs: 
  - Corporate profile request (RFP/RFI facts): 1 business day turnaround via DealDesk Portal (DDP-01).
  - Standard due diligence packet (SOC2, ISO 27001 letters, financials): 2 business days via Secure Document Vault (SDV-SEC17).
  - Executive briefing scheduling: 5 business days lead time; 30-minute format.
- Data resiliency: Corporate knowledge base RPO: 4 hours; RTO: 2 hours (SDV-SEC17 + Snowflake).
- Do:
  - Use approved statements in Disclosures (Section 7).
  - Anchor origin story to our client-first risk culture and track record.
  - Confirm legal entity and regulator when referencing licenses.
- Don’t:
  - Don’t imply guaranteed returns, deposit insurance for non-deposit products, or unlimited payment guarantees.
  - Don’t share non-public financials outside SDV-SEC17 controlled access.

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases

- Institutional CIO/CTO: Needs comfort on vendor stability, control environment, and scale. Use for onboarding NFG as a manager, lender, or payments provider.
- Corporate Treasury: Evaluates cash management, payments resilience, and cross-border capabilities; often requests company lineage and regulatory perimeter.
- UHNW Family Office: Prioritizes fiduciary heritage and conflict management for trust/estate solutions.
- Middle-Market CFO: Assesses lending capacity/history through cycles.
- Regulators/Auditors (client-side): Require evidence of compliance history and incident management maturity.

Eligibility fit:
- Fit: Clients valuing regulated, multi-jurisdictional providers with strong risk frameworks and audited controls.
- Non-fit: Startups requiring rapid, experimental product changes or unregulated crypto custody.

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions

BANT/CHAMP prompts:
- Budget: “Do you have vendor qualification thresholds tied to regulatory status or financial ratios (e.g., debt/equity, liquidity)?”
- Authority: “Who signs off on vendor risk? What artifacts do they need (SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS)?”
- Need: “Which part of NFG’s portfolio are you considering, and which licenses matter for your use case?”
- Timing: “When is your go-live? Do you require an executive briefing or site visit before final approval?”
- Challenges: “Any prior issues with providers related to outages, data sovereignty, or audit exceptions?”
- Metrics: “How will you measure successful onboarding (SLA, error rates, uptime)?”

Red flags:
- Requests to bypass due diligence, refusal to use SDV-SEC17, claims of guaranteed returns requirement, pressure to misstate regulatory coverage.

# 4. Product/Policy Overview

Company mission: Provide transparent, regulated financial services with operational excellence.

Lines of business:
- NFG-INV: Discretionary and non-discretionary investment advisory; 1,200 advisors; SEC Form ADV on file with CRD 194021.
- NFG-LEND: Asset-based lending, equipment finance, and cash-flow loans; OCC-chartered bank entity NFG Bank N.A.
- NFG-TRUST: Corporate trustee, estate administration; chartered trust company in Delaware and Channel Islands.
- NFG-PAY: Domestic and cross-border payments, virtual accounts, treasury APIs; PSD2-compliant in EU via NFG Payments Ltd.

Compliance-by-design:
- Enterprise policies: NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival (immutable records), NFG-KYC-0110 (Customer Identification), NFG-BCP-200 (Business Continuity), NFG-INFOSEC-27001 (ISMS).
- Systems of record: 
  - SFDC-NA12 for CRM, 
  - Archer GRC for risk,
  - SDV-SEC17 for artifacts,
  - Snowflake for analytics (restricted, tokenized PII).

Historical milestones:
- 1987: Founded as Northbridge Investment Counsel in Boston.
- 1996: Launch of Commercial Lending unit; rebrand to NFG.
- 2003: Acquisition of Harbor Trust Co. (expansion into fiduciary services).
- 2012: NFG Payments Ltd established in London (FCA 742619).
- 2020–2022: Cloud migration to Snowflake and Kafka microservices; ISO 27001 certified.
- 2023: Surpassed $750B in client assets advised; zero material audit findings for the third consecutive year.
- 2024: Launched real-time cross-border rails (NFG-PAY/Instant).

# 5. Pricing, Fees, and Discounting

Corporate-level fee posture:
- NFG-INV: Advisory fee bands (tiered AUM).
- NFG-LEND: Interest margin over benchmark; arrangement fees.
- NFG-TRUST: Schedule-based trustee fees; special asset surcharges.
- NFG-PAY: Per-transaction fees + monthly platform fee.

Standard fee bands (illustrative):
- NFG-INV Advisory:
  - 0–$25M AUM: 65 bps
  - $25–$100M: 50 bps
  - $100M+: 35 bps
- NFG-PAY:
  - Platform: $2,500/month
  - Domestic ACH: $0.08/txn
  - Cross-border SWIFT: $9.00/txn

Discount guardrails:
- Max discretionary discount without approval: 10% off card for any unit.
- Multi-product bundle: Up to additional 5% with SVP approval and 24-month term.

Approval Matrix (Discounting)
- Level 0: Up to 10% — Owner: Sales Director; Evidence: Quote-0001 in SFDC; SLA: 4 hours.
- Level 1: 10.01–20% — Owner: VP Sales + Finance BP; Evidence: Margin Analysis (DDP-01); SLA: 1 business day.
- Level 2: 20.01–30% — Owner: Deal Desk + BU CFO; Evidence: Competitive Justification, Win-Loss notes; SLA: 2 business days.
- Level 3: >30% or negative unit margin — Owner: CRO + Legal; Evidence: Strategic Deal Memo; SLA: 5 business days; Escalation: CRO Office.

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow

Use for any external request referencing NFG history, stability, or licenses that could impact risk evaluation or pricing.

Steps:
1) Intake (SLA: 4 business hours)
- Submit DDP-01 intake with RFI/RFP ID (e.g., RFI-2025-1147).
- Required artifacts: Client name (CUST-12345), opportunity (OPP-98111), use case summary, due date, requested statements.
- Control check: Validate client is in SFDC with KYC stage ≥ “KYC-Prelim”.

2) Artifact Assembly (SLA: 1 business day for standard packets)
- Pull latest SOC2, ISO 27001, financial highlights, org charts from SDV-SEC17.
- Control check: Verify document versions and dates; must match “current” flag in SDV.

3) Legal/Compliance Review (SLA: 1–2 business days)
- Route to Legal and CCO queue in Archer.
- Control check: Redline any custom statements; map to approved language library (LIB-CORP-OVW).

4) Executive Briefing (optional)
- Schedule within 5 business days; assign Exec Sponsor (SVP+).
- Control check: Briefing deck approved by Strategy (v# logged).

5) Delivery & Logging
- Deliver via SDV secure link; set access TTL to 14 days.
- Control check: Record in SFDC “Due Diligence Completed” date; attach DDP-01 case.

Escalation path:
- If SLA breach imminent, escalate to Deal Desk Manager within 2 hours; beyond 24 hours, to VP Sales Enablement; regulatory-sensitive items escalate to CCO immediately.

# 10. Procedures

A) Provide Company Overview Packet to Prospect
1) Confirm Opportunity alignment (SFDC product codes). Control: Lines_of_Business__c must match.
2) Submit DDP-01 intake with due date. Evidence: DDP-Case-008912.
3) Retrieve LIB-CORP-OVW current via API or SDV. Control: Version must match latest.
4) Assemble packet (company history, licenses, SOC2 letter, ISO certificate). Evidence: SDV package link.
5) Legal review if custom language requested. Control: Archer ticket COMMS-REQ-4421 approved.
6) Deliver via SDV link. Control: Access TTL set; verified recipient email domain.
7) Log completion in SFDC field “Due_Diligence_Completed__c”. Evidence: timestamp.

B) Executive Briefing Request
1) Propose 3 time slots ≥5 business days out. Control: Exec calendar check.
2) Draft 5-slide deck: Overview, History, Risk, Products, Case study. Control: Strategy sign-off (STR-APP-2213).
3) Rehearse talk track (15 mins). Evidence: Enablement session record.
4) Host briefing; capture Q&A in SFDC Notes. Control: No off-script commitments.
5) Send follow-up with SDV link and action items. Control: All items assigned in SFDC Tasks.

C) Regional License Confirmation
1) Identify intended services and client domicile.
2) Check “Regulatory_Licenses__c” and Regional Variations (Section 14).
3) If ambiguity, open Legal inquiry ticket. Control: No statements to client until confirmation.
4) Update SFDC with confirmed entity. Evidence: Legal response attached.
