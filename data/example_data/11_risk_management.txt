# 1. Quick Reference (TL;DR)
- Who: Frontline advisors, RMs, underwriters, and support engaging any customer with credit, deposit, payments, or capital markets exposure > $100,000.
- What: Apply NFG risk rating, exposure limits, covenants, and ongoing monitoring per RiskHub workflows; capture evidence in Polaris CRM.
- When: 
  - Pre-sale qualification before proposal.
  - Pre-commitment credit decision (underwriting).
  - Post-close monitoring monthly/quarterly.
- Where: Polaris CRM for customer data; Aurora for underwriting; RiskHub for ratings/limits; Gatekeeper for approvals; Nimbus DocVault for artifacts.
- SLAs:
  - Initial risk pre-check: 4 business hours.
  - Full risk assessment (EAR): 2 business days standard; 8 business hours expedited (fee).
  - Deal Desk approval turnaround: 1 business day for Level 1–2; 2 days for Level 3+.
- Do:
  - Run KYC/AML screening before sharing term sheets (NFG-KYC-0110).
  - Use the Exposure and Approval Matrix in Section 5.
  - Record all exceptions and mitigating controls.
- Don’t:
  - Promise pricing or limits before risk rating is assigned.
  - Email PII/financials; upload to Nimbus only (NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival).
- Escalation: Underwriter of record → Risk Manager (Region) → ERM Duty Officer (24x7: +1-415-555-0198).

# 2. Customer Personas and Use Cases
Personas:
- Middle-Market Corporate CFO (Revenue $50M–$500M): Needs revolving credit, FX hedging, cash management.
- Fintech COO (Venture-backed): Needs sponsor bank services, payment flows, safeguarding.
- Institutional Asset Manager (AUM $1B+): Needs custody, securities lending, leverage.
- Cross-border Importer/Exporter: Needs trade finance, letters of credit, sanctions screening.

Use Cases:
- New Revolving Credit Facility (RCF) up to $50M with springing covenants.
- Payment processing for marketplace platform with embedded wallets.
- Securities-backed line of credit with daily margining.
- Supply chain finance for international vendors.

Eligibility Fit:
- Audited financial statements or reputable review.
- Transparent ownership to UBO level; passes KYC/AML and sanctions checks.
- Industry within NFG risk appetite (no prohibited industries list NFG-RA-100).

Non-Fit (Redirect/Decline):
- Cryptomixers, unlicensed MSBs, sanctioned counterparties.
- Opaque ownership structures or PEPS without enhanced due diligence (EDD) feasibility.
- Highly leveraged balance sheet with interest coverage < 1.5x absent collateral/guarantees.

# 3. Qualification and Discovery Questions
BANT/CHAMP-style prompts:
- Budget: “Have you allocated fees for risk assessment or collateral appraisal? Typical fees range $2,500–$25,000.”
- Authority: “Who signs the borrowing resolution? Is Audit Committee approval required?”
- Need: “What risk objective are you solving—liquidity, FX, settlement, operational resilience?”
- Timing: “Any covenants or maturities within 120 days?”
- Challenge: “Any pending litigation, regulatory inquiries, or data breaches in the last 24 months?”
- Metrics: “Target leverage, DSCR, and liquidity thresholds you manage to?”
- Process: “Can we obtain bank/broker statements, AR aging, and customer concentration detail?”

Red flags:
- Refusal to disclose UBO to 25% threshold.
- Last-minute pressure to issue term sheet before KYC complete.
- Material weakness disclosure without remediation plan.
- Rapid growth with negative operating cash flow and reliance on related-party financing.

# 4. Product/Policy Overview
Capabilities:
- Risk rating engine (RiskHub v3.2) producing Obligor Risk Rating (ORR 1–10) and Facility Risk Rating (FRR A–E).
- Exposure Limits module setting Single Obligor Limit (SOL) and Country Risk Limit (CRL).
- Covenant Monitoring service (RM-COV-201) with monthly/quarterly triggers.
- Automated Risk Alerts (RM-ALR-415) via EventBridge for covenant breaches and sanctions hits.
- Exception Management with documented mitigating controls and sunset dates.

Limitations:
- Startups < 12 months of operating history require EDD and sponsor support.
- Cashflow-negative facilities > 24 months tenor require collateral or guarantees.
- Sanctions screening uses 15-minute batch windows; real-time hits require manual confirmation.

Dependencies:
- Polaris CRM customer master (CUST_ID).
- KYC via Lexicon KYC API and internal NFG-KYC-0110 workflow.
- Financial spreading in Aurora Underwriting (FS-Template-03).

# 5. Pricing, Fees, and Discounting
Standard fees (USD; regionally adjusted, see Section 14):
- Risk Assessment (RM-SVC-101): $4,500 standard; $9,500 complex (consolidations > 3 entities).
- Expedited Assessment SLA (8 hours): +$2,000.
- Covenant Monitoring (RM-COV-201): $750/month per obligor (includes 3 covenants).
- Additional Covenant: $125/month.
- Annual Limit Review (RM-LMT-310): $2,000.

Discounting guardrails:
- Max discretionary discount per deal: 15% (Sales Director).
- Bundled services (3+): up to 20% with Risk Manager approval.
- No discount on expedited fee without Level 2 approval.

Approval Matrix:
- Level 0: List price. Owner: RM/Advisor. Documentation: None. SLA: Immediate.
- Level 1: ≤15% discount; ≤$25M exposure; ORR ≤ 6. Owner: Sales Director + Underwriter. SLA: 1 business day.
- Level 2: 16–25% discount; $25M–$75M exposure; ORR 7–8 or FRR D. Owner: Regional Risk Manager. SLA: 2 business days.
- Level 3: >25% discount; >$75M exposure; ORR 9–10; any prohibited exception. Owner: ERM Committee. SLA: 3 business days.

# 6. Deal Desk Workflow
Step-by-step:
1) Pre-Check (Sales/RM)
- Action: Create Opportunity in Polaris (OPP-YYYY-####). Run KYC pre-screen.
- Evidence: Screenshot of KYC “Pass” uploaded to Nimbus DocVault under folder /OPP-####/KYC.
- SLA: 4 business hours.

2) Risk Intake (Underwriter)
- Action: Submit Exposure Assessment Request (EAR) via RiskHub.
- Required artifacts: 3 years financials, AR/AP aging, cash flow, org chart, UBO attestations.
- SLA: 2 business days standard.

3) Rating and Limits (RiskHub)
- Action: Generate ORR/FRR, set SOL/CRL, propose covenants.
- Control: Automated cross-check with Atlas Data Lake for industry PD/LGD benchmarks.

4) Pricing/Terms Draft (Advisor + Underwriter)
- Action: Build term sheet using Section 12 template.
- Control: Check limit utilization vs SOL in RiskHub.

5) Approvals (Gatekeeper Deal Desk)
- Action: Route per Approval Matrix; attach EAR (EAR-2025-####).
- SLA: Level-based. Escalation auto-triggers if > SLA by 4 hours.

6) Customer Negotiation (Advisor)
- Action: Present terms. Document objections and resolutions in Polaris Activity log.

7) Close & Book (Operations)
- Action: Final KYC/AML, sanctions check, covenant baseline capture, document execution.
- SLA: 1 business day post-approval.
- Control: Sentinel DLP scan before archival in Nimbus.

Escalation Path:
- Stuck > SLA: Page Risk Duty Officer via Gatekeeper “Escalate” button.
- Disagreements on rating: Submit Reconsideration Ticket (RRT-####) to ERM Analytics.

# 7. Risk and Compliance Disclosures
Required verbal statement (pre-term sheet):
- “NFG’s credit and risk terms are subject to Know Your Customer, anti-money laundering, and sanctions compliance and may change based on our risk assessment.”

Required written disclosure (term sheet footer):
- “Facility availability is contingent upon ongoing compliance with covenants and NFG’s risk policies NFG-KYC-0110, NFG-AML-0205, and NFG-RA-100. NFG may suspend availability upon material adverse change or sanctions designation.”

Recording/archival:
- All customer communications affecting risk terms must be logged in Polaris and archived per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival (7-year retention, WORM storage).

# 10. Procedures
A) Onboarding Risk Assessment (RM-SVC-101)
1. Create EAR in RiskHub. Control: Mandatory KYC_STATUS in Polaris must be Pass or EDD.
2. Collect financials and documents via SecureLink (no email). Evidence: Nimbus upload receipt.
3. Spread financials in Aurora (FS-Template-03). Control: Two-person review for adjustments > $500k.
4. Assign ORR/FRR. Evidence: RiskHub rating snapshot exported to Nimbus.
5. Set SOL and proposed covenants. Control: Compare to Atlas PD/LGD benchmarks (±2 sigma).
6. Draft term sheet; route through Gatekeeper per Section 5. Evidence: APP-#### stored.
7. Communicate terms with required disclosures. Evidence: Polaris Activity log with timestamp.
8. Book facility post-approval. Control: Sanctions re-check within 24 hours of booking.

B) Ongoing Covenant Monitoring (RM-COV-201)
1. Configure covenant definitions in RiskHub. Evidence: JSON config stored in Nimbus.
2. Ingest customer data monthly/quarterly via SFTP or API. Control: Checksum validation; Sentinel DLP scan.
3. Evaluate covenants. If breach, auto-create Case in Polaris (CASE-####).
4. Notify RM and Underwriter via risk.alerts.covenant_breach. SLA: RM must contact client in 1 business day.
5. Remediation plan documented; set deadline (≤30 days) or trigger default remedies. Evidence: Case notes.
6. Close case with evidence (financials, waiver approval). Control: Second-line QA sample 10% of cases monthly.

C) Exceptions Handling
1. Identify exception (e.g., leverage > policy). Flag EXCEPTIONS_FLAG in Polaris.
2. Propose mitigating controls (e.g., additional collateral, guaranty, pricing add-on).
3. Submit Exception Memo to Gatekeeper with sunset date. Approval per Level 2+.
4. Track exceptions dashboard weekly. Control: Auto-expire alerts 10 days before sunset.

D) Sanctions/AML Escalation
1. Potential hit received. Booking blocked automatically.
2. AML Investigator review within 4 hours. Evidence: Case AML-#### in Atlas.
3. If confirmed, decline/exit per NFG-AML-0205. Notify Legal and ERM. Document SAR if required.
4. If cleared, update SANCTIONS_STATUS=Clear; proceed with booking.

# 11. Objection Handling Matrix
- “The fee for risk assessment is too high.”
  Response: “Our fee reflects the accelerated SLA and two-person review. Bundling monitoring reduces total by up to 20% with approval. We can also scope standard SLA at $4,500.”

- “We can’t provide audited financials.”
  Response: “We can consider reviewed statements with a parent guaranty or higher collateral coverage. This would route to Level 2 approval.”

- “Why do you need UBO information?”
  Response: “Regulations require us to verify ownership to prevent sanctions and AML risk. We only collect what’s necessary and store it in WORM-compliant Nimbus per NFG-SEC-17a-4-Archival.”

- “We need terms today.”
  Response: “We can expedite the assessment within 8 business hours for $2,000 and start with a preliminary view, subject to KYC completion.”

# 13. KPIs and Dashboards
Leading:
- Time to Initial Risk Pre-Check (target ≤ 4 hours). Owner: Sales Ops.
- EAR Cycle Time (target ≤ 2 days standard, ≤ 8 hours expedited). Owner: Underwriting.
- Covenant Data Timeliness (percent on-time submissions, target ≥ 95%). Owner: RM.

Lagging:
- Covenant Breach Rate (≤ 8% of portfolio per quarter).
- Non-performing Exposure (NPE) ratio (≤ 1.5%).
- Exceptions with expired sunset (0%). Owner: ERM.

Quality/Compliance:
- QA Exceptions Rate in onboarding (≤ 5%).
- KYC Rework Rate (≤ 3%).

Dashboards:
- RiskOps in Polaris Analytics: tiles for SLA adherence, breach severity heatmap, exceptions aging.
- ERM Committee Board: exposure vs SOL/CRL, ORR migration, stress loss at 95% VaR.

# 14. Regional Variations
US:
- GLBA governs customer data; SAR filing requirements under BSA/FinCEN. Use OFAC lists.
- State usury caps may influence pricing add-ons.

UK/EU:
- GDPR data minimization; ensure purpose limitation in KYC data retention.
- FCA SYSC requires robust governance and outsourcing oversight for third-party data providers.

CA:
- OSFI Guideline B-10 for third-party risk management. PIPEDA for privacy.

APAC:
- MAS 637 credit risk; data residency for SG personal data; use MAS sanctions lists.

Fees:
- EU VAT may apply to monitoring services.
- FX hedging add-ons require EMIR reporting in EU.
